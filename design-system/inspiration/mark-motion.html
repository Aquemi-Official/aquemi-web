Brand mark · loading and intro

Five ways to animate the mark

Each option borrows from a familiar loading indicator and rests on the supplied mark: the dots stay on their grid and the wave keeps its shape. Shown at 120 px on a light ground, on navy, at 24 px beside a loading label, and in the lockup with the wordmark, which stays still while the mark moves. All loop here; any of them can also play once as an intro.
