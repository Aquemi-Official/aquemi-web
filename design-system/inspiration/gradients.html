Gradient backdrops

Eight ways to give a section depth without adding objects. Each is built only from the colour tokens, so it follows light and dark themes, and each keeps body text on a solid enough ground to hold 4.5:1.

The brand book currently says "No gradients." Adopting one of these means amending that rule, ideally to a narrow allowance: one gradient ground per page, on a band or hero, never behind tables or long text.
