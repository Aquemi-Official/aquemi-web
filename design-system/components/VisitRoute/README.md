VisitRoute lays a visit's checks out as numbered stops on one route line drawn on a Plate, each stop a line drawing of the equipment it covers, for a walk-round whose order matters, such as what a preventive maintenance visit covers, ending at the sign-off.

- An Icon name in `spot` draws at the spot size (112 px desktop, 76 px phones, `ink-muted`) so every stop reads at the same visual size.
- Data: a `grid` block with `"as": "route"`. Items keep `{t, d, link}` and take `spot`: a Spot name (`assets/spots/`), the stop's vignette. Spots are all or none; without them the stops are bare numbered tags on the route. `"end": true` on the last item makes it the sign-off (a filled tick tag, a `flow` frame round its figure and a "Sign-off" caption). No `fold`: a route shows every stop.
- Markup (`blocks/grid.js`): `wPlate(…, {cls:"aq-visit-route__plate"})` (a `line` frame on `surface-raised`) around `ol.aq-visit-route` (`--cols`; `aq-visit-route--spots` when every stop has a spot) of `li.aq-visit-route__stop` (`--c`, `--r`; `is-ltr`/`is-rtl`, `is-in`, `is-turn`, `is-last`, `is-end`), each an `a` or `div.aq-visit-route__body` with `span.aq-visit-route__fig` (aria-hidden: Spot `aq-visit-route__spot` and the square tag `.aq-visit-route__n`), `span.aq-visit-route__cap` (end stop only), `h3.aq-visit-route__t`, `p.aq-visit-route__d`. DOM order is the route order.
- Desktop: the plate on the page ground; rows of 3 or 4 stops (4 when the count divides into fours or leaves three), odd rows left to right, even rows right to left, joined by square U-turns in the side gutters. The route runs through the middle of the figures (160 px vignettes) and stops at their edge. Phones: still on the plate, one vertical route down the left through 96 px vignettes, the name and line beside each.
- Tokens: route a 1 px `line-strong` line; vignettes sit on `surface-raised` so the line stops at their edge, with no corner brackets; vignettes `ink`, `flow` on hover of a linked stop; tags 12 px mono `flow` in a 1 px `flow` square (`radius-sm`), the end tag filled; the "Sign-off" caption in the `eyebrow` style; names Geist 500 17/24 sentence case `ink` (16/24 on phones); lines `ink-muted` 15/24; linked names carry the `flow` arrow like LinkGrid.
- Ground: the page `surface`; the plate follows the page theme. Order the stops as the engineer walks them (data first, then along the water path, sign-off last). Pick each spot for the thing checked at that stop. For a project timeline use ProcessSteps; for the water's own path use WaterPath.

## Motion

Motion (`motion.js`): the route is walked stop by stop. Each stop's frame opens, its vignette wipes in, the numbered tag pops onto the corner and the text rises; then the dashed route runs on to the next stop (`--aq-run` scales the run, `--aq-turn` opens the U-turn and a right-to-left row's lead-in; both default to 1 in the CSS) and the next stop pops as the line arrives. The sign-off tick lands last. Desktop: one timeline under 4.2 s; phones: each stop as it scrolls in, queued behind the line. Hover lifts a linked stop's tag.
