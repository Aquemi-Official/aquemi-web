Seven system families

Each page gives limits, design parameters, options and documents. Or send your user requirement specification (URS) and we map the whole path.

  1. Purified Water (PW) generation

    Reverse osmosis (RO) and electrodeionization (EDI) skids: PW below 1.3 µS/cm at 25 °C and 500 ppb total organic carbon (TOC), hot water sanitizable.USP <645>

  2. WFI by distillation

    Multi-effect and vapour compression stills making hot WFI at < 0.25 EU/mL endotoxin.USP <85>

  3. WFI by membrane

    Double-pass RO, EDI and ultrafiltration (UF): ambient WFI without plant steam for generation.Ph. Eur. 0169

  4. Pure steam generators

    Condensate that meets WFI limits; steam that passes EN 285 at the sterilizer.EN 285

  5. Storage and distribution loops

    Turbulent flow at peak draw, branches at L/D ≤ 2, hot WFI loops above 70 °C, ambient loops sanitized by heat or ozone.ASME BPE

  6. Pretreatment

    Media, softening and carbon or bisulfite: no detectable chlorine or chloramine at the RO inlet, silt density index (SDI) ≤ 3.USP <1231>

  7. Monitoring and controls

    Online TOC and conductivity on the raw reading; audit trails that meet 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11.21 CFR Part 11
