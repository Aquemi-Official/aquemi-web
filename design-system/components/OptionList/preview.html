Configurations and options

  1. 01

    Single or double pass RO

    Double pass where feed is high in dissolved solids or where the same skid feeds a still.

  2. 02

    Hot water sanitizable

    Heat-tolerant membranes and a sanitization heat exchanger for 80 °C cycles.

  3. 03

    Integrated pretreatment

    Softener, carbon or bisulfite dosing mounted on the same skid where space is short.

  4. 04

    Variable speed pumps

    Constant permeate flow as feed temperature and membrane fouling change.

  5. 05

    Duplex trains

    Two trains at 50 or 100 % each, so one runs while the other is serviced.

  6. 06

    Ultrafiltration or UV

    Downstream ultrafiltration or 254 nm UV where your microbial target needs more margin.

  7. 07

    Recovery enhancement

    Concentrate recycle or a reject RO to reduce water sent to drain.

  8. 08

    Instrumentation

    Online conductivity at each stage, optional online TOC at the skid outlet.

Decide before your requirements (URS) are approved

  1. 01?

    Product mix in five years

    Generation and headers sized for planned lines, expansion written into the URS.

  2. 02?

    USP, Ph. Eur. or both

    Both is cheap at design and costly later.

  3. 03?

    Loop sanitization route

    Natural polypropylene (PP) is rated 0 to 80 °C, and its pressure rating falls as it heats.

  4. 04?

    Canadian feed water

    Near freezing in winter, over 20 °C in summer; membranes sized for the coldest month.

  5. 05?

    Shutdown windows

    Duplex trains and isolable sections let you service without stopping every line.
