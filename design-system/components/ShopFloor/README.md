ShopFloor places the company's people or roles where their work happens, as numbered stations on one isometric plan of the shop with a walking route through them, so a reader sees the shop and who works where before reading the cards.

- On the site: a `grid` block (About, "The people you would work with") or a `table` block (Careers, "Roles, base and pay") with `"look": "shop-floor"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-shop-floor.js`). Origin: concept `design-system/inspiration/gemini/61-concept-shop-route.png` (the shop floor as a navy isometric with numbered stations and a route).
- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy` → `div.aq-shop-floor` (`is-long` with more than three cards) → `figure.__fig` (aria-hidden: `div.__plan` with the IsoKit `svg.aq-iso--navy` and `span.__pin` number tags placed with `isoPct`) and `ol.__cards` of `li.__card[data-st]` → `span.__n`, `div.__body` (`h3.__t`; grid: `p.__name`, `p.__d`, `p.__go` link; table: `dl.__dl` of `div.__f` with the column name as `dt`).
- The plan: floor slab, perimeter walls, partitions with the aisle open, and seven stations (`g.__st[data-iso-id]`): `design` (drafting board), `front` (binders), `qa` (binders and stamp), `fab` (skid frame with a PP spool and valve), `test` (RO rack), `ctl` (control panel), `dock` (truck with a crated skid). Stations no card uses are drawn quieter (`is-idle`). The walking route (`g.__walk`, a bold `on-navy` dash so it reads apart from the muted walls) runs from the shop door along the aisle (kept clear of the partitions) to each used station in card order; each leg is an IsoKit pipe without walls whose `to` is its station.
- Fields: grid `h`, `intro`, `items [{t, d, link, station}]`; table `h`, `intro`, `cols`, `rows`, `stations [..]` (one per row). A grid item's `d` that opens with a bracketed name line (`[Full name] · [licence or qualification].`) shows that line as the nameplate, the rest as the text. All copy through `tx`; placeholders stay chips (tinted for the navy ground).
- Interaction: hovering or focusing a card lights its station and walking leg (`is-lit`, flow) and fills its pin; on wide screens with long lists, the card crossing 45 % of the view does the same as you scroll (the plan is sticky).
- Tokens: `navy` band, `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted` text and lines, `limestone` lit pin, Geist Light titles and numbers, Geist Mono field labels and pins.
- Phones: the plan is a band above the cards (not sticky), pins keep their numbers, fields stack.
- Not for process trains or equipment explanations (use SystemRun, StrataRun); not for a sequence of steps (ShopRoute is the fabrication sequence as a floor plan).

## Motion

Motion: the shop is drawn, then walked. The slab, walls and each station draw on back to front through `AQiso.timeline`, the route is laid, then a drop walks it from the door station to station; each station lights as it is reached, its pin drops in and its card's top rule runs across. Then the plan hands over to the reader (hover, focus, scroll). Cards slide in from the right as they reach the view. Plan under 4.5 s; nothing under reduced motion (the plan and every pin are complete without script).
