LimitGauges shows a block of water or steam quality limits as quiet instrument faces on a plate, one dial per limit, so a reader sees at a glance where each limit sits and which side of it passes.

- Data: a `spec` block with `"as": "gauges"` (rows `{p, v, m, hl}` as for SpecTable). Renderer: `wGauges` in `assets/src/blocks/spec.js`. The list sits on a Plate (`wPlate(..., {block: ['Aquemi', caption]})`): a `line` frame on `surface-raised` with a quiet caption. It follows the page's theme; nothing forces light or dark.
- Markup: `.aq-plate > ul.aq-limit-gauges > li.aq-limit-gauges__item` (`is-key` for an `hl` row, `is-readout` when there is no dial). Each cell: `h3.aq-limit-gauges__p` (parameter, Geist 500 16/24, sentence case), `.aq-limit-gauges__dial` (`aria-hidden`), `.aq-limit-gauges__foot` with `dl.aq-limit-gauges__spec`: "Limit" (`dd.aq-limit-gauges__v`, the full value as written) and "Basis" (`dd.aq-limit-gauges__m`, standards as `.aq-tag`, the words between them in `.aq-limit-gauges__mt`). The dial and its readout are decoration; in a dial cell the "Limit" line is visually hidden (`.aq-limit-gauges__sr`, still read by screen readers) because the label and readout show it. The rest of the value around the readout's number and unit ("@ 25 °C (stage 1)", "(≤ 4.3 µS/cm @ 20 °C)", "(500 ppb)", or a unit too long for the readout) is shown as the readout's caption (`.aq-limit-gauges__cap.is-q`, mono 12 px, `ink-muted`, wraps) in place of "Upper limit"; the regulatory qualifiers always stay visible.
- The dial draws, all in single-weight 1 px strokes: a `line-strong` scale arc with ticks and scale numbers, the side that passes as a `flow` line inside the arc (below the limit for ≤ and <, above it for ≥ and >), an `ink` mark and small pointer on the scale at the limit with a "Limit ≤ 1.3" label (mono 12, the word in Geist `ink-muted`), a hairline needle from a small pivot resting on the limit, and bottom right a mono readout (the limit with its unit, "Upper limit" or "Lower limit" under a `line` hairline). No crosshairs, registration marks or filled chips. A unit longer than 8 characters ("mL per 100 mL") is left out of the readout; the spec list has it.
- Numbers used: only the row's own number, the first `≤ / < / ≥ / >` number in `v` outside `[placeholders]`, or a value that opens with a number (an action level, read as an upper limit). **The scale is derived from that limit alone**: 0 to the next round number (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 8 × 10ⁿ) at least 20 % above the limit (5 % for ≥ and >, so a 0.95 dryness reads on 0 to 1). The scale numbers are a ruler, not data; scale numbers next to the pointer are dropped so the label stays readable.
- No measured value exists, so the needle rests on the limit; it never shows an invented reading. No number (a placeholder, "Not required") gives a readout cell: the same cell without the dial, the value in larger mono between `line` hairlines.
- Second marker: a row named "outlet" or "guaranteed" whose value has a real number in the same unit as a limit adds a dashed `flow` mark at its place on that dial's scale, with its number in `flow`. A placeholder adds nothing; the row stays a readout cell.
- Tokens: each cell opens on a `line` hairline (no frame); scale, ticks, needle and pivot `line-strong`; scale numbers mono 10 viewBox units `ink-muted`; pass line `flow`; limit mark `ink`; label mono 12 `ink` on the plate ground; readout Geist Mono 300 24/28 `ink`, caption Geist 12/16 `ink-muted` in sentence case; basis Geist 12/18 `ink-muted`; the `hl` cell's top hairline is `flow`. Nothing is uppercase.
- Exactly four (or seven) instruments run four across, so no row has empty cells. Titles balance-wrap.
- Desktop: three instruments across, the dial (up to 280 px) straight under the title, readout mono 24 px. Phone: two compact instruments across (dial about 135 px): no scale numbers (they collide with the arc at a legible size), every label 12 px or more, the limit label without its "Limit" word, the readout (mono 18 px) under the dial, the basis as its standard tags only. Readout cells sit in the grid like any other cell.
- Supports `fold` and `fold_noun` (default "limits"), but limits should not be folded.
## Rule variant (`as: "rules"`)

Design rules that are not release limits (L/D ≤ 2, return velocity ≥ x, sanitization temperature ≥ x) on the same plate, as compact horizontal threshold bars (sys-storage "Loop design rules"). Renderer: `wRules` in `assets/src/blocks/rules.js`.

- Markup: `.aq-plate > ol.aq-limit-gauges.aq-limit-gauges--rules > li.aq-limit-gauges__rule` (`is-key` for `hl`, `is-text` when there is no threshold): `.aq-limit-gauges__rn` (the shared mono index, `aria-hidden`), `h3.aq-limit-gauges__p`, `.aq-limit-gauges__rv` (the bar, then `.aq-limit-gauges__rt` the rule as written), `.aq-limit-gauges__m` basis with standard tags.
- Bar (`.aq-limit-gauges__bar`, `aria-hidden`): a 1 px `line-strong` track without ticks, the threshold as a 2 px `ink` mark with its value label above (mono 12 `ink`, the sign and value; kept inside the bar), the passing side as a `flow` line on the track (right of the mark for ≥, left for ≤), scale ends in mono when the threshold is a number (0 to the next round number, `gaugeScale`: a ruler, not data).
- Threshold: item field `rule` (`"≥ [design minimum return velocity, m/s]"`, `"≤ 2"`), else the value: a ≤ < ≥ > sign or "at least / minimum / no less than" (≥), "at most / maximum / no more than / up to" (≤), followed by a number with its unit or a `[placeholder]`. A placeholder draws the bar unnumbered with the mark at mid-scale and the placeholder as its label (`is-open`): the shape of the rule, the value still to come. Never put a number in `rule` the page does not state.
- A rule without a threshold (a part, a method) keeps its row, the value in Geist 16/24, so the sheet reads as one list.
- Desktop: index | name | bar and rule text | basis, rows between `line` hairlines (`flow` above the `hl` rule). Phone: index and name on one line, the bar full width, the rule text and basis tags under it. Supports `fold`, `fold_noun` (default "rules").

- Use it for limits with numbers (conductivity, TOC, endotoxin, EN 285 steam tests, RO inlet targets). Design rules: the rule variant above. Design parameters stay a SpecTable, or ParameterCallouts when they name parts of the equipment.

## Motion

- No measured value exists, so the needle rests on the limit; it never shows an invented reading. No number (a placeholder, "Not required") gives a readout cell: the same cell without the dial, the value in larger mono between dashed rules. A value made only of placeholders ("[outlet conductivity guarantee, µS/cm @ 25 °C]") also gets an **open dial** (`.aq-limit-gauges__dial--open`, item `is-open`, desktop only): the bare instrument, dashed `ink-muted` arc, `ink-subtle` ticks, no numbers, pointer, band or chip, the needle at rest on zero, so the empty frame reads as an instrument fitted but not yet set. Text values ("Not detectable") never get one.

## Motion

`motion.js`: each instrument powers up once on scroll-in, cells in a row a beat apart. The scale arc draws, the inner arc and crosshairs follow, the ticks pop on from zero to full scale, the scale numbers light, the pivot seats; the needle sweeps up from zero and settles on the limit (elastic) while the readout counts up with it; on arrival the limit tick and pointer flash once in `flow`, the chip drops in, the pass band fills, a guaranteed outlet marker and the basis tags follow (under 3 s). Open dials draw their scale and the needle twitches and returns to rest. Rule rows: the row rules across, its number decodes, the scale wipes on, the threshold mark drops through the track and flashes, the passing band fills away from it, then the chip, the rule text and the tags. Folded rows are not animated. Phones: the same, 20 % shorter, no caption decode.
