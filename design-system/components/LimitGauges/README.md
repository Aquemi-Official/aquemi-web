LimitGauges shows a block of water or steam quality limits as a panel of small instruments, one dial per limit, so a reader sees at a glance which side of each number passes.

- Data: a `spec` block with `"as": "gauges"` (rows `{p, v, m, hl}` as for SpecTable). Renderer: `wGauges` in `assets/src/blocks/spec.js`. Ground: a light sheet (`gauges` is in `LIGHT` in `page.js`).
- Markup: `ul.aq-limit-gauges` of `li.aq-limit-gauges__item` (`is-key` for an `hl` row, `is-readout` when there is no dial): `h3.aq-limit-gauges__p` (parameter), `.aq-limit-gauges__dial` (`aria-hidden`: an SVG arc and the `.aq-limit-gauges__mark` chip), `p.aq-limit-gauges__v` (the full value, as written), `p.aq-limit-gauges__m` (the basis, standards as `.aq-tag`).
- The dial uses only the row's own number: the first `≤ / < / ≥ / >` number in `v` outside `[placeholders]`, or a value that opens with a number (an action level, read as an upper limit). The limit always sits at three quarters of the arc; the side that passes is the `flow` band (below the limit for ≤ and <, above it for ≥ and >). The scale carries no other numbers, so nothing is invented. No number (a placeholder, "Not required") gives a readout cell: the same cell without the dial.
- Second marker: a row named "outlet" or "guaranteed" whose value has a real number in the same unit as a limit adds a dashed `flow` mark on that dial, in proportion, with its number on an outlined chip. A placeholder adds nothing; the row stays a readout cell.
- Tokens: hairline grid in `line` on `surface-raised`; scale and ticks `line-strong` (major ticks `ink-muted`); limit mark `ink` with an `ink` chip and `surface` text; parameter in mono caps 12 px; value in mono 16 px; basis in sans 13 px `ink-muted`; `hl` row has a 2 px `flow` top rule.
- Desktop three instruments across; phones two across, compact (dial 84 % of the cell, value mono 13 px, basis 11 px).
- Supports `fold` and `fold_noun` (default "limits"), but limits should not be folded.
- Use it for limits with numbers (conductivity, TOC, endotoxin, EN 285 steam tests, RO inlet targets). Not for design parameters or rules with few numbers: those stay a SpecTable, or ParameterCallouts when they name parts of the equipment.
