GradeLadder reads a grade table beside one sticky instrument: the row's number set huge and light, a fader up the water grades, and a minimum line at the row's minimum grade; the row in hand (or, while scrolling, the row on the reading line) drives it.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"as": "grades"` and `"look": "grade-ladder"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-grade-ladder.js`, `window.AQladder.show`); used on ind-pharma (grade by dosage form). Concept: pass 9b ind-pharma.1 (vertical grade scale with a minimum limit line and a big numeral beside the table). Replaces GradeMains.
- Markup: `div.aq-grade-ladder` (`--nl` lanes) → `div.__inst` (aria-hidden, sticky; `--f` 0–1 is the minimum's height): `div.__read` (`span.__k`, `span.__num`, `span.__step`, `span.__tag` with `__k` and `__val`), `div.__fader` (`span.__labels` with `span.__mark[data-k]` per lane (`__lt`, `__ln`; `.is-off`, `.is-min`, `.is-alt`), `span.__ruler` of `i` ticks, `span.__track` with a `span.__zone` per grade not supplied and `span.__fill`, `span.__limit` (`.is-open` dashed)); then the fold with `div.__list` → `p.__colh`, `ol.__rows` of `li.__row[tabindex=0]` (`data-i`, `data-f`, `data-k`, `data-alt`, `data-g`; index, `h3.__t`, `p.__g` with a visually hidden key, `p.__note`).
- Fields: `cols`, `rows` [step, grade, note], `intro`, `fold`, `fold_noun` (phones only). Added: `lanes` [{id, t, short, note, off}] (bottom to top; `short` is the scale label, `off` a grade we do not supply, drawn as an open dashed zone with its `note`), `taps` [["pw", "wfi?"], …] per row (lane ids; `?` = an alternative the note names, shown as an open mark; with no plain id the line sits at the lowest alternative, dashed). WFI appears only as the grade above PW that Aquemi does not supply.
- Tokens: page ground (core alternation), track `surface-raised` with `line-strong`, fill `flow-tint`, minimum line and the minimum's label `flow`, numeral Geist Light 232/184 `ink`, grades Geist Mono. Selected row `flow-tint` with a 2 px `flow` edge; focus ring `focus`. Arrow keys, Home and End move between rows.
- Phones: the instrument is a sticky band over the rows (number left, the fader lying flat), the fold returns.

## Motion

Entrance once: ticks run up the scale, labels set in, fill and minimum line rise to the first row's grade, rows rise. Then the page's one scroll-linked story: the row on the reading line (45 % of the viewport; phones just under the band) is shown stepwise; the fader glides 380 ms (.22, 1, .36, 1) and the number ticks in (8 px, 220 ms). Ports of Subframe scroll-turntable (stepwise indexed view in a sticky panel) and elastic-fader (oversized level number, fill scaled from its foot; its overshoot and stretch dropped). Reduced motion: no scroll story; the row in hand still shows, instantly.
