What the assessment reviews

  1. 01 / 06

    Records

    Qualification file, changes, drawings, dead leg register.

  2. 02 / 06

    Water quality data

    A year of conductivity, TOC and microbial results.

  3. 03 / 06

    Generation

    Performance on today’s feed, capacity at your coldest feed.

  4. 04 / 06

    Storage and distribution

    Materials, dead legs, slope, flow and fittings.

  5. 05 / 06

    Sanitization

    Whether your results support the schedule (WHO TRS 1033, 8.4).

  6. 06 / 06

    Controls

    Support status, audit trail and backups.
