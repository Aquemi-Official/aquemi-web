How water moves through the skid

  1. 01 / Feed

    Softened, dechlorinated feed

    Arrives from pretreatment free of chlorine and hardness, so the membranes last.

  2. 02 / Heat

    Heat exchanger

    Where fitted, warms winter feed to the membrane design temperature.

  3. 03 / RO 1

    RO pass 1

    Reverse osmosis (RO) removes most dissolved ions, organics and bacteria.

  4. 04 / CO₂

    CO₂ removal

    Strips or converts carbon dioxide, which passes RO as a gas.

  5. 05 / RO 2

    RO pass 2

    Where needed, adds margin on conductivity, TOC and silica.

  6. 06 / DI or EDI

    DI or EDI

    Removes the remaining ions: electrodeionization (EDI) without regeneration chemicals, deionization (DI) by [DI resin: service exchange or on-site regeneration].

  7. 07 / Out

    To PW storage

    Off-spec water diverts until it recovers.
