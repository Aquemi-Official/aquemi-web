NumeralIndex sets a router table as a Swiss index of situations, each under a huge light outline numeral with its start page as a square go-cell, so a reader finds their situation by scanning large type rather than a table.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"as": "router"` and `"look": "numeral-index"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-numeral-index.js`); used on ind-pharma. Origin: pass 9, no concept of its own (quota); adapted from services.0 (giant light outline type) and the STYLE-ATLAS "measured editorial" spine.
- Markup: `ol.aq-numeral-index` of `li.__row` → `span.__rule` (hairline that draws), `span.__n` (Geist Light 128/104 outline numeral, `-webkit-text-stroke` in `line-strong`, aria-hidden), `div.__body` (`h3.__t` Geist Light 32/40, `p.__need` per middle column with a mono `span.__k` label), and `a.__go` (the last column's link: mono label, page icon, the page name, a drawn arrow).
- Fields: `cols`, `rows` [situation, need…, {t, link, intent}]. A last cell without a link renders as a dashed, flat cell.
- Hover/focus: the rule fills with `flow`, the numeral fills `flow`, the go-cell turns `navy` with a limestone icon. Section heading Geist Light 56/60.
- Phones: numeral 72 px over the row, need stacked, go-cell full width.

## Motion

Rows follow one another down the index: each rule draws from the left, the numeral rolls up out of its slot while its digits decode (ScrambleText), the situation and need rise in, the go-cell wipes open from the left. Once on scroll-in, under 4 s; nothing under reduced motion.
