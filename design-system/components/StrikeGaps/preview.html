Data integrity gaps in older controls

PLC-5, SLC 500, S5 and S7-300 are discontinued or in phase-out: spares are limited.

GapRequirement it missesWhat the upgrade does
  1. 01

    Gap: Shared operator login, or none

    Requirement it misses: 21 CFR 11.10(d); Annex 11, 12

    What the upgrade does: Individual accounts, roles, automatic logoff

  2. 02

    Gap: No audit trail on set points or alarms

    Requirement it misses: 21 CFR 11.10(e); Annex 11, 9

    What the upgrade does: Audit trail of who, what, when and why

  3. 03

    Gap: A chart recorder as the release record

    Requirement it misses: 21 CFR 211.68; Annex 11, 4

    What the upgrade does: Validated historian, or a defined paper record

  4. 04

    Gap: Backups never restored

    Requirement it misses: Annex 11, 7.2

    What the upgrade does: Restore test at OQ, repeated on a schedule

  5. 05

    Gap: Records only in the old system

    Requirement it misses: 21 CFR 211.180; Annex 11, 17

    What the upgrade does: Verified migration, or old records kept readable
