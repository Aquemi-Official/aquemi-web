ContactRoutes sets the ways into Aquemi side by side as cards, each with its own next steps (1 → 2 → 3) and response clock, so a visitor on a contact page sees which route fits before the form: request a proposal, production stopped (call the service line), or visit the shop.

- Data: block `"type": "contact-routes"` with `routes: [{t, d, steps: [3 strings], clock, link, phone?, urgent?, current?, action?, intent?}]`. `link` is a page id. `clock` is the full response line ("Written reply within [proposal response time, working days]"); every time stays the page's own placeholder until Aquemi confirms it. `action` overrides the button label (default: the linked page's nav label); `intent` ticks options on that page's form, as on Button.
- Current route: `current: true` (or `link` equal to the page being read) frames the card in `flow` on `flow-tint`, marks it "This page" (`aria-current="page"`) and turns its action into a jump to this page's form (the form block's `nav` id; without one, the first form on the page). The other cards link to their pages with a secondary button.
- Urgent route: `urgent: true` puts the card on `limestone` (`data-theme="light"`, so every token inside resolves light) with an "Urgent" marker and a `fail` dot, and sets `phone` large (28 px, 24 px on phones) under a phone icon; once the number is real it becomes a `tel:` link. The urgent card is first in the markup, so phones show it first and it is never hidden; desktop keeps the data order through `--o`. One urgent route per block.
- Markup (`blocks/contactroutes.js`): `ul.aq-contact-routes` (inline `--routes`) of `li.aq-contact-routes__route` (`is-current`, `is-urgent`, inline `--o`): `.aq-contact-routes__mark` (with `.aq-contact-routes__dot`), `h3.aq-contact-routes__t`, `.aq-contact-routes__d`, `.aq-contact-routes__phone` (`.aq-contact-routes__icon`, `.aq-contact-routes__k`, `.aq-contact-routes__num`), `ol.aq-contact-routes__steps` (`.aq-contact-routes__n` aria-hidden, `.aq-contact-routes__s`), `.aq-contact-routes__clock` (icon, `__k` "Response", `__v`), `.aq-contact-routes__action`.
- Desktop: equal columns, `line-strong` frames, `radius-sm`, padding `space-8`/`space-6`; steps are numbered rings joined by a `flow` riser; the action sits at the foot of every card. Phones (`@container (max-width: 860px)`): cards stack with `space-6` between, urgent first; the mono "Call" and "Response" labels move to screen readers only and the icons carry them.
- Ground: the navy page (a decision anchor), right after the hero on contact pages, so the form still starts within two phone screens. Keep it to three routes and three short steps each (one line on a phone).
- Not for a list of related pages (RelatedLinks) or for choosing a technical route by situation (Router).
