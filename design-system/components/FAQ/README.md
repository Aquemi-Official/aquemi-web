FAQ answers the questions engineers and QA ask before they call, using native disclosure.

- Markup: `<div class="aq-faq">` with one `<details><summary>question</summary><p>answer</p></details>` per item.
- Questions are full sentences in the reader's words. Answers lead with the direct answer, then the standard or number.
- Four to eight items per page. Don't hide anything a buyer needs in order to decide.
