Questions we hear

01Can you supply only one part of the system?

Yes. We supply each family on its own and design the interfaces, such as feed water quality to a still or return temperature to a tank, so it integrates with equipment already on your site.

02How do we size generation against storage?

We start from your point-of-use demand profile over a full production day. Generation covers the average draw plus sanitization and recovery time, and the tank covers the peaks. We share the calculation with you.

03Do you build to a standard platform or custom?

Both. Standard configurations shorten lead time and are prequalified at FAT. We then adapt materials, instrumentation and controls to your URS. [Confirm platform names and lead times].
