What each stage issues

Your proposal lists your system's subset.

  1. Stage

    01Specify and designAnnex 15, 3.3

    Documents issued

    • URS compliance matrix
    • Basis of design
    • Risk assessment
    • P&IDs
    • Functional specification
    • Design review report
    • C&Q plan
    • Traceability matrix

    Your step, and who approves

    Design qualification (DQ). Your quality unit approves DQ; your engineering lead, drawings.

  2. Stage

    02FabricationAnnex 15, 3.9

    Documents issued

    • Fabrication and inspection plan
    • Material log and certificates
    • Welder qualifications
    • [PP joint records kept]
    • Pressure test and cleaning records

    Your step, and who approves

    Installation qualification (IQ) evidence. Both parties sign the inspection plan; you verify records at IQ.

  3. Stage

    03FAT and SATAnnex 15, 3.6; GUI-0029, 8.1

    Documents issued

    • Approved protocols
    • Executed protocols with raw data
    • Deviation log
    • FAT release decision
    • SAT report
    • Code review
    • Software version record

    Your step, and who approves

    Tests your quality unit agrees to reuse in IQ and OQ. It approves each protocol before execution.

  4. Stage

    04QualificationAnnex 15, 3.8–3.14

    Documents issued

    • IQ and OQ protocols and reports
    • Dead leg register and slope survey
    • Sanitization records
    • PQ protocol, sampling plan, phase reports
    • Proposed alert and action levels

    Your step, and who approves

    IQ, OQ and PQ. Your quality unit approves each protocol and report.

  5. Stage

    05Handover

    Documents issued

    • Document register and punch list
    • As-built drawings
    • Calibration certificates
    • Software backup
    • Operation and maintenance manual
    • Draft procedures
    • Spare parts list

    Your step, and who approves

    Your system owner accepts the package.

  6. Stage

    06Service and changeAnnex 15, 4.1 and 11; GUI-0029, 7

    Documents issued

    • Signed service reports
    • Calibration certificates
    • Annual system review
    • Updated as-builts
    • Change-scoped test records

    Your step, and who approves

    Periodic review; changes go through your change control.
