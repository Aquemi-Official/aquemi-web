How the controls are built

  1. 01

    Instruments

    Measure total organic carbon (TOC), conductivity and temperature online.

  2. 02

    Controller (PLC)

    Runs sequences, interlocks and divert, even if every layer above fails.

  3. 03

    Operator panel (HMI)

    Where operators see alarms, trends and sanitization status.

  4. 04

    Supervisory control (SCADA) historian

    Keeps time-stamped records with an audit trail.

  5. 05

    Plant network

    Reaches your network through a demilitarized zone (DMZ).
