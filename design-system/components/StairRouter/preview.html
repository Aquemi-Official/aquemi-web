Where to start

  1. 01

    New system

    Your situation
    New system, user requirement specification (URS) approved or in draft
    Start with
    Process design: request a URS gap review
    What to send
    Your URS, feed water analysis, peak demand in L/h
    What comes back
    A compliance matrix marking every gap, within [URS review turnaround, working days]
  2. 02

    Design approved

    Start with
    Fabrication: request a spec review
    What to send
    Approved drawings, piping and material specification, province
    What comes back
    Clauses we meet or would change, a draft inspection plan, within [document review turnaround, working days]
  3. 03

    Skid in build

    Your situation
    Skid in build, factory and site acceptance tests (FAT, SAT) ahead
    Start with
    FAT and SAT: request a specimen FAT protocol
    What to send
    Your work email
    What comes back
    Test cases with acceptance criteria, within [specimen package response time, working days]
  4. 04

    Installed

    Your situation
    Installed or arriving, qualification ahead
    Start with
    Commissioning and qualification (C&Q): request a plan review
    What to send
    URS, validation master plan, points-of-use list
    What comes back
    Draft C&Q and sampling plans, within [document review turnaround, working days]
  5. 05

    In service

    Your situation
    In service and qualified
    Start with
    Service: request a plan quote
    What to send
    System, age, instruments, standby and ride-through time
    What comes back
    Plan, annual price, first-visit tasks, within [quote response time, working days]
  6. 06

    Aging

    Your situation
    Aging, out of support, or about to change
    Start with
    Upgrades: request a system assessment
    What to send
    Drawings, a year of water data, the problem
    What comes back
    Scope and price within [assessment response time, working days], then options with downtime and requalification
  7. 07

    Comparing suppliers

    Start with
    Quality: request a supplier audit
    What to send
    Your supplier questionnaire
    What comes back
    Your questionnaire completed, within [audit request response time, working days]
