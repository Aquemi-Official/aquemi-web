RoleLoop sets a table of roles as the bays of one twin-wall pipe loop on the navy band: the loop meanders over one role card and under the next and runs back on the second row, and each role's branch off the loop carries a valve that reads open or closed only when the role's own copy says so.

- On the site: Careers, "Roles, base and pay" (`table` block, `"look": "role-loop"`; renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-role-loop.js`, which also lays out the pipe). Origin: pass 9b concept `research/pass9b/concepts/careers.0.png` (role cards set inside the bends of one double-line pipe loop with valves).
- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy` → `div.aq-role-loop` → `svg.__pipe` (aria-hidden: `g.__segs` one `path.__seg` per bay, `path.__wall`, `path.__bore`, `path.__water`, `g.__stubs` one `g.__stub.is-open|is-closed|is-unset` per role with wall, bore, water, valve body `__vb` and stem `__vs`) and `ol.__bays` of `li.__bay[data-i][data-valve]` → `p.__no` (aria-hidden "01 / 06"), `h3.__t` (the role), `p.__st.is-<state>` (small valve `svg.__vk` and the status text), `dl.__dl` of `div.__f` (`dt` column label, `dd` value).
- Fields (a `table` block): `h`, `intro`, `cols` [role, what you do, must have, base and travel, pay and opening], `rows` of strings. The last cell is "pay · status": the part after the last " · " is the status. Its valve reads open when that part begins "Open", closed when it begins "Closed"; anything else (a `[placeholder]`) draws the valve dashed with no water in the branch, so no status is implied. Best for 1 to 6 roles (one or two rows of three).
- Layout: the pipe is computed from the rendered cards (ResizeObserver): verticals in the column gaps and outside the outer columns, horizontals a quarter row gap above or below the cards, alternating per bay; two rows close on themselves, one row returns along a lane under the cards. Without it the cards stand alone.
- Tokens: `navy` ground and bore, `on-navy` walls at 62 %, `flow` water (3 px core) and the lit bay, `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted` text, Geist Light 26 role names, Geist Mono for numbers, labels and status.
- Phones (container under 860 px): the cards stack; the loop runs down their left and back up their right, each branch off the left run.
- Not for comparing roles column by column (CompareTable) or for a long list of postings (a register).

## Motion

Motion: the twin wall draws round the cards, then the water runs the loop at one steady rate (ported from Subframe Motion "Score follow", score-follow: the linear playhead at a set tempo, the note under it turning the accent); as it passes a role's branch that bay lights for a beat, the branch shows (water to the valve, through it only when the copy says open) and the card prints in. Once, under 4.5 s. Pointing at a role lights its bay and fills it from end to end in 0.4 s; it fades a third faster. Nothing under reduced motion.
