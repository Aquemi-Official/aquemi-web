AuditManifold sets an evidence table (who reads, what they read, where we answer it) on a navy blueprint sheet whose column joins are pipe headers, so each reader's row reads as one route from the auditor to our answer.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"as": "evidence"` and `"look": "audit-manifold"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-audit-manifold.js`); used on ind-meddevice ("Who audits your water system"). Origin: pass 9b concept ind-meddevice.3 (auditor table on a navy blueprint grid, double-line manifolds with valves feeding each row across the columns).
- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy.aq-audit-manifold-sec` → `svg.__grid` (blueprint grid, aria-hidden) → `.aq-in` → section head → `div.aq-audit-manifold` → `p.__colh` (column heads, aria-hidden: the phone shows them as `span.__k` keys per cell) and the fold (`wFold`) holding `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` (`div.__who` with `span.__no` and `h3.__t`; `span.__pipe--a`; `div.__read` with `p.__v` in Geist Mono; `span.__pipe--b`; `div.__go` with `a.__link`). Each `span.__pipe` (aria-hidden) is a twin-wall header `span.__hdr`, a branch `span.__br`, a diaphragm valve `svg.__valve` and a flange `span.__fl`; `i.__w` is the water line in each.
- Fields, as the evidence table: `h`, `intro`, `cols` [who, read, answer], `rows` [who, read, {t, link, intent} or text], `fold`, `fold_noun`. Every link, intent and the fold are kept.
- Tokens: `navy` ground, `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted` text and pipe walls, water `flow` mixed toward `on-navy`; Geist Light 22 for the auditor, Geist Mono 15 for what they read. The header starts at the first row's centre and ends at the last row's centre; a folded table's header runs on toward the "Show all" button.
- Rows: pointing at or focusing a row (its link) marks it `is-on`: its valves fill with water and their stems turn a quarter (open), its branch water brightens, the other rows step back to 62 % opacity.
- Phones: the column heads go; each row stacks the auditor over what they read and where we answer it, with a short vertical riser in a left gutter teeing into both.
- Not for: a table without a reader → document → answer route; use a plain evidence table or ProofFolders.

## Motion

Ported from Subframe Motion "Animated workflow diagram" (process-choreography): one progress value drives overlapping segments. The first header fills down row by row, each branch fills as the header passes it and its "what they read" brightens, then the second header and its branches, and each answer arrives as its branch reaches it (opacity, 6 px rise, 0.96 → 1 scale, cubic ease-out). One run on scroll-in (2.4 s), then the inline styles are cleared. Hover or focus on a row re-runs its two branches (0.18 s each) while the valves open in CSS. Nothing under reduced motion; the static table is complete without script.
