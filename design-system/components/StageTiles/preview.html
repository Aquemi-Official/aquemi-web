What each stage proves

One commissioning and qualification (C&Q) plan covers every stage.

  1. 01/

    Commissioning

    Flush, pressure test, sanitization, start-up, punch list.

  2. 02/
    FAT · SAT

    Factory and site acceptance tests (FAT and SAT)

    Tested before shipping, then on your utilities.

  3. 03/

    Installation qualification (IQ)

    Installed as designed: drawings, materials, joints, slopes, instruments.

  4. 04/

    Operational qualification (OQ)

    Operating limits and worst case tested.

  5. 05/

    Performance qualification (PQ)

    Required quality, reproducibly, across the seasons.

  6. 06/

    Periodic review

    Yearly review of data and changes; requalify where they point.
