FaultKnot sets "when something goes wrong" as an editorial split: a huge light title running across a navy and limestone ground, a knotted twin-wall pipe that loosens step by step, and the fault steps read across the foot with the call step on the navy.

Look `fault-knot` (`assets/src/blocks/look-fault-knot.js`) on a `steps` block. Used on svc-service, "When something goes wrong". Concept: research/pass9b/concepts/svc-service.5.png (headline crossing the colour split, a knotted-pipe figure, five slash-numbered steps, the call step on navy).

- Markup: `section.aq-band--limestone.aq-fault-knot-sec[data-theme=light]` > `div.aq-fault-knot[style=--n]` > `__ground` (the navy field, aria-hidden), `__top` > `__head` (`__ground2` for phones, the real `header.aq-sec-head`, `__twin`: the same heading as `p.aq-h1`, aria-hidden, clipped to the navy) + `figure.__fig > svg.__svg` (`__frame`, `__grid`, three `__p` paths sharing one d: `__wall`, `__bore`, `__flow`; `__flange`), then `ol.__steps > li.__step[data-i][data-amp]` (`is-call` on the first) > `h3.__t` (`__n` "01 /" aria-hidden, the name, a `__go` link when the step has one) + `p.__d`. Steps without a link take `tabindex=0`.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `items [{t, d, link}]`. Each step's knot tension (`data-amp`) runs from 1 (tied) at the first step to 0 (straight) at the first step whose text says the water is back ("back within"), else the last.
- Script: `fkD(amp)` returns the pipe's path (220 points, the same command structure at every tension so it can morph); `fkSet(look, amp, step)` sets it (the d attribute and the CSS `d` property) and lights the step's number; pointing at or focusing a step calls it.
- Tokens: section limestone, field `navy`, title 300 at up to 136 px in `navy` (twin in `on-navy`), pipe walls `navy` with the bore in `limestone`, water `flow`, frame `line-strong`, grid `line`; the call step in on-navy, its placeholders as dashed chips.
- Phones: the navy field sits behind the top of the title and the split runs through its second line; the drawing full width under it; the call step a navy row; the other steps stacked on hairlines.
- Don't use it for steps with hold points (ProcessSteps, CutoverSchedule) or for more than six steps.

Motion: ported from Subframe Motion "SVG shape morph" (shape-morph): the pipe's d transitions 0.7 s cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) when a step is pointed at or focused (keyboard lands at once), and the water runs the straight pipe once (2.4 s). Entrance (motion.js): the title lands word by word in both colours together (the core's word reveal, run here on the heading and its twin so words stay whole across the split), the frame fades up, the knot inks itself in (1.8 s), the steps rise. Then the page's one scroll-linked story: as the section scrolls through the viewport the knot loosens step by step and runs straight at the step that brings the water back; the step in reach lights its number. No pin; nothing under reduced motion (the tied knot stands; a pointed step changes it at once).
