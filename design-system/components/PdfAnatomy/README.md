PdfAnatomy sets delivery terms as a figure plate of the delivered document: a PDF open in a viewer (bookmark pane, a search hit, the number and revision box in the footer of every page of the stack) with each part of the highlighted row's value as a callout on a leader, and the other rows beside it as signature lines or a large statement.

- On the site: a `spec` block with `"look": "pdf-anatomy"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-pdf-anatomy.js`), cmp-documentation "Delivery and signatures". Origin: no concept of its own; the style atlas's best pairing (a line drawing and its data joined by leaders: 54-concept-explainer-drawing, 77-p9-systems-3). Pass 9b added the drawing-sheet frame from research/pass9b/concepts/cmp-documentation.3.png (its table was not followed).
- Markup: `div.__sheet` > `div.__frame` (aria-hidden: `__zb--t/b/l/r` zone bands, numbers 1-4 across, letters D-A down; `__tb` title block: the section title over blank `No.` and `Rev.` fields), the section head, then `<div class="aq-pdf-anatomy">` > `figure.__plate` (`figcaption.__cap` > `__k`, `h3.__h`, `__note`; `__draw` > `__crop` > `__art` > `svg.__svg` + `__pin`s; `ol.__calls` > `li.__call[data-c]`; `nav.__pager[data-p][data-n]` > `button.__pgs[data-d]`, `__pgw` > `__pgr[--pg-start]` > `button.__pgn[data-p][aria-current]`) and `__side` > `__row` (`h3.__h`, `__k`, `ul.__sigs` or `p.__big`, `__m`).
- Fields: `cols [item, how we deliver it, note]`, `rows [{p, v, m, hl, link, link_label, parts}]`. The first `hl` row (else the first) is drawn; its value splits at commas into callouts, each finding its part by its words (search, bookmark, else number/revision/page = the footer) or by `parts` (`"search"`, `"mark"`, `"foot"`). Another row whose value has ";" becomes signature lines, one per choice ("or" kept as a small mono word); any other value is set large.
- Tokens: plate `surface-raised` line art in `line-strong` / `line`, what a callout points at in `flow` / `flow-tint`, the logo bar `navy`; callouts 16 px sans; side values 300 weight.
- Hover: pointing at a callout draws its leader in flow.
- Page turner (renderer script; ported from Subframe Motion "Sliding pagination", compact-pagination): the drawn document has 12 pages, one per bookmark. Previous/next and page buttons (44 px) turn it: a five-wide window of numbers slides to keep the page in view (250 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), only the visible numbers tabbable), the current bookmark and the search hit move, the front page turns (230 ms), the counter at the foot of the bookmark pane updates, and the footer's number and revision boxes stay put and light: the same on every page. Instant under reduced motion.
- Phone: the plate is cropped to the viewer with numbered pins, the callouts follow as a numbered list, the page turner centred, then the other rows; the frame's band narrows, the title block runs the sheet's width.
- Motion (`motion.js`): the viewer frame draws, pages fan from under the front page, text prints, the hit and footer boxes light, leaders run out to their callouts; side rows rise, signature lines draw. Under 3.5 s.
- Not for: specifications with numbers (SpecTable, ParamTiles).
