Minimum water grade by dosage form

Minimums from the EMA water guideline (EMA/CHMP/CVMP/QWP/496873/2018, in effect 1 February 2021). Your product file can set a higher grade, never a lower one.

Product or process stepPotablePWWFI
  1. 01

    Parenteral products, including biologics and vaccines

    Also haemofiltration, haemodiafiltration, peritoneal dialysis and irrigation solutions

    WFI

  2. 02

    Sterile ophthalmic, nasal, ear and cutaneous preparations

    Your risk assessment may call for a higher grade to protect sterility

    PW

  3. 03

    Oral preparations and nebuliser solutions

    WFI for sterile, non-pyrogenic products such as cystic fibrosis nebulisers; non-parenteral vaccines by risk assessment

    PW

  4. 04

    Non-sterile cutaneous, nasal, ear, rectal and vaginal preparations

    Potable water only where justified, for some veterinary products

    PW

  5. 05

    Granulation and tablet coating

    Water that leaves the product during drying still needs PW

    PW

  6. 06

    Formulation before sterile lyophilization

    PW before non-sterile lyophilization

    WFI

  7. 07

    Final rinse of equipment, containers and closures

    PW for non-parenteral products; WFI for sterile parenterals

    Same grade as the product

    = product
  8. 08

    Active substance synthesis before final isolation

    PW where the chemistry needs it; PW or WFI for final isolation

    Potable water
