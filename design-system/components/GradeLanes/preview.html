Minimum water grade by product and process step

Minimums from the EMA water guideline (EMA/CHMP/CVMP/QWP/496873/2018, Tables 1, 3, 4, 5), in effect since 1 February 2021. Most biologics plants use WFI for media and buffers anyway.

Product typePotablePWWFI
  1. 01

    Biologics, including vaccines and ATMPs, finished product

    Excipient in the final formulation

    WFI

  2. 02

    Biologic or fermentation product, not a vaccine or ATMP

    Fermentation and cell culture media

    Potable; PW where greater chemical purity is needed

  3. 03

    Vaccines, and ATMP starting materials sterilized later, such as viral vectors

    Fermentation and cell culture media

    PW

  4. 04

    ATMPs not sterilized later, such as cell-based products

    All steps: media, initial purification, final isolation and purification

    WFI

  5. 05

    Active substance in solution, not sterile, for parenteral use

    Before, then at final isolation and purification

    PW, then WFI

  6. 06

    Sterile parenteral products

    Final rinse of equipment, containers and closures, including CIP

    WFI

  7. 07

    Sterile products

    Initial rinse, including CIP

    PW

  8. 08

    Any product, sterile lyophilized

    Formulation before sterile lyophilization

    WFI
