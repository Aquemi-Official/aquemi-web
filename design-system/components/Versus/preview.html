03Feed water
PW: the first column runs hot enough to scale on silica and hardness

04Capital and operating cost
More effects cost more to buy and less steam to run
Compressor adds purchase cost; runs cheaply where power costs less than steam
05Moving parts
Feed pump only
Compressor, with bearings and seals to maintain
06How it works
Plant steam boils PW in column 1; each column's vapour heats the next and condenses as WFI
A compressor raises the vapour's pressure so it condenses on the surface that boils more feed

Choose Multi-effect when

Reliable plant steam at enough pressure and spare boiler capacity

Choose Vapour compression when

Steam is costly or limited, cooling water scarce, or power cheap, as in several provinces
