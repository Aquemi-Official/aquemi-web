Versus sets two routes face to face across a spine of factors, for a buyer choosing one of two (hot or ambient WFI, multi-effect or vapour compression).

- Data: a `table` block with `"as": "versus"` and exactly three columns: `cols` [factor label, route A, route B], `rows` [factor, A, B]. A row whose factor starts "Fits when" or "Choose" becomes the verdict.
- Markup (`blocks/versus.js`): `.aq-versus` > `.aq-versus__head` (two `.aq-versus__name` and the `.aq-versus__vs` "or"), `dl.aq-versus__rows` of `.aq-versus__row` (`dt` factor, two `dd` with `data-k` = route name), then `.aq-versus__verdict` with "Choose A when" and "Choose B when".
- Drawn on a Plate (`wPlate`: a `line` frame on `surface-raised`, caption Aquemi · nav · N factors). Desktop: the route names at `h2` (Geist 400 28/34, sentence case; B in `flow` as a word), joined by a `line` hairline to a plain "or" (Geist 16/24 `ink-muted`) on the spine; each factor sits on the `line` spine (`.aq-versus__n`, the shared mono index in `ink-subtle`, then the factor in Geist 500 16/24) with hairline leaders out to A (right-aligned) and B (left-aligned, `flow` leader); answers Geist 16/26. The verdict is two columns under hairlines (`line-strong` for A, `flow` for B), each led by an `eyebrow`. Phones: names and "or" on top, the verdicts next, then the spine runs down the left edge with each factor's index on it, A and B under the factor, each tagged with its route (Geist 500 12/18) and ruled on its left.
- Conclusion (block field `conclusion`: `[label, A, B]`, or a string for one line): a closing line that is not a choice, such as what carries into IQ/OQ (svc-fat-sat). Drawn as a footer band across the plate under the factors and the verdict (`.aq-versus__end`): `surface-sunken` under a `line` hairline, the label as an `eyebrow` on the spine, A right-aligned and B left-aligned in Geist 16/24. A string (or a one-cell array) spans the band under an "Either way" label. Phones: the label, then A and B each ruled and tagged with its route, like the factors.
- Ground: the section's `surface` with the plate on `surface-raised`, in either theme. `ground: "light"` is still accepted (the renderer adds `.aq-versus-plate--light`) but no longer changes anything. Keep each cell to one line of fact (≤ 25 words). Three or more routes: CompareTable. No boxes, round nodes or uppercase names.
- Supports `fold: N` (first N factors show; the verdict always shows). Put the deciding factors first.

## Motion

`motion.js`: the "or" node pops on the spine and the two route names slide in from either side; the spine then draws down factor by factor (a clip on the rows), each node lighting as the line reaches it and its two answers pushing out to their sides; only then does the verdict land, and the conclusion band opens from the spine outwards. Phones: names, then the verdict (it sits on top there), then the spine runs down the left edge one factor at a time. Under 5 s; nothing under reduced motion.
