GateLine draws a short process as one double-line pipe on a navy band: each step owns a run of pipe with a line icon above and its number, name and line below, and a gate valve sits on every joint between steps.

Look `gate-line` (`assets/src/blocks/look-gate-line.js`) on a `steps` block. Used on svc-upgrades, "How an upgrade runs". Concept: pass 9b svc-upgrades.4 (the pipe as the process axis, valves as stage gates, one hairline icon per stage); pass 9's idea of your system's water broken only at the planned shutdown is kept.

- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy.aq-gate-line-sec[data-theme=dark]` > `p.__legend` (aria-hidden: water swatch and the lane label) + `ol.aq-gate-line` > `li.__step[.is-cut][.is-hold]` (`__ic` icon, `__run` aria-hidden with `__walls`, `__water` or `__dry` > `__dl` label, `__gate` > `svg.__vsvg` (`__vb` bowtie, `__vfill` closed fill, `__vs` stem, `__vh` handwheel) and `__gk` "Hold" tag, `__out` arrow on the last; `__txt` > `__n`, `h3.__t`, `p.__d`, `p.__hold`).
- Fields: `h`, `items [{t, d, hold, icon, link}]`, `lane`. The step whose line names a (planned) shutdown gets the dry run, labelled with those words. A step with `hold` gets the hold stamp ("Hold · Signed by …") and a "Hold" tag on the gate after it. New optional `icon` per step (a `wIcon` name); `lane` labels the water in the legend.
- Tokens: pipe walls `on-navy-muted` 1.5 px, water `flow`, dry bore dashed `on-navy-muted`, valves `on-navy` strokes on `navy`; numbers Geist 300 40 px, names 300 22 px, lines 14 px `on-navy-muted`; tags mono 11 px on a `flow` frame.
- Phones: one column; the pipe runs down the left (the valves turned with it), icon, number, name and line on the right. The run labels and gate tag are dropped (the step text names them).
- Not for long processes (more than six steps) or steps with durations: use ProcessSteps or HoldTimeline.

Motion: every gate starts shut; the water fills the pipe run by run, each gate opening (fill clears, handwheel turns) as the water reaches it; at the gate after a hold step the water waits while the hold stamp strikes and the gate's tag lands; the shutdown run stays dry while its dashed bore prints. Each step's text rises as its run fills. Once, about 4 s; phones per step. Hover (mouse, after the entrance) seeks the water to the pointed step: runs ahead drain and their gates shut, nearest first; leaving refills (port of Subframe Motion cue-scrubber).
