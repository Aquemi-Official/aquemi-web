WasherTrace tells an explainer as the water's way from the loop into a washer: one IsoKit line drawing on a limestone band, the points as callouts on leaders from their parts, and a navy readout that shows where along the route the water has reached.

- On the site: a `prose` block with `"as": "explainer"` and `"look": "washer-trace"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-washer-trace.js`); used on ind-meddevice ("Where rinse water reaches the device"). Origin: pass 9b concept ind-meddevice.1 (loop-to-washer schematic with three numbered stations under a navy flow readout); its invented flow figure is replaced by the reached point.
- Drawing (IsoKit, `aq-iso--limestone`): the loop comes in at floor level to a pump (`loop`), rises into a header with a diaphragm valve and drops into the washer through a ball-valve sample point (`inlet`), then runs into the wash chamber (`chamber`): the washer cabinet, its chamber, baskets and spray arm are drawn in the renderer from IsoKit primitives (the kit has no washer part).
- Markup: `div.aq-washer-trace` → `div.__fig` (`div.__draw` with `svg.aq-iso.__svg` holding the scene and `g.__leads`, plus `span.__pin` numbers for phones; `div.__read` aria-hidden: `div.__ruler` with ticks, `span.__mark` per point at its place along the route and `span.__cur` at `--reach`, `p.__big` with `span.__rn` and `span.__of`, `p.__tag`) and `ol.__pts` of `li.__pt` (`span.__n`, `h3.__t` holding `button.__go`, `p.__d`), placed at their leader ends with `--x` / `--y`.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `points` [{t, d}] (the first three take the stations in order). `figure` and each point's `at` are not used by this look (they stay in the page file for the default renderer).
- Tokens: `limestone` ground, `on-limestone` / `on-limestone-muted` text and lines, water `flow` mixed toward `on-limestone` (reads in both themes); readout `navy` with `on-navy`, the reached number in Geist Mono Light 104.
- Without script, under reduced motion: the whole route is drawn, every callout shows, the readout shows the last point.
- Phones: the readout on top, the drawing cropped to the equipment with numbered pins, the points as a numbered run under it.

## Motion

On scroll-in the drawing builds the IsoKit way (parts back to front, pipes, leaders) and the callouts rise; then the water runs from the loop to the chamber once, each station lights on arrival, the readout's cursor rides the water's reach and its numeral and tag tick over (quote-tick, 0.22 s). Then the points drive it, ported from Subframe Motion "Chapter timeline scrubber" (cue-scrubber): pointing at or focusing a point's title seeks one reach value (0.5 to 0.9 s by distance, power2.inOut) so the water drains back or runs on to that station, stations ahead go dark, the readout follows; leaving runs it back to the chamber. Entrance under 5 s, nothing loops, nothing under reduced motion.
