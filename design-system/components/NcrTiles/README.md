NcrTiles shows how a defect is held and closed as a bento of navy and limestone tiles, each led by a huge light slash numeral, the hold step oversized, with one tile drawing the repair step in isometric line art.

- On the site: a `steps` block with `"look": "ncr-tiles"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-ncr-tiles.js`), cmp-quality "How a defect is held and closed". Origin: pass 9b concept `cmp-quality.1` (six NCR steps as navy/limestone tiles with slash numerals, a dimensioned joint-repair drawing tile). Replaces CapaRing.
- Markup: `<ol class="aq-ncr-tiles">` (12-column grid, 1 px `line` gaps) > `li.__tile.is-navy|is-stone` (`is-lead` first, `is-wide` second when there is no drawing, `is-hold`, `is-drawn`; `--o` phone order) > `__n` (aria-hidden "01/"), `h3.__t`, `p.__d`, `p.__hold` (closed-valve `svg.__valve`, "Hold · Signed by …" from `hold`); the drawing tile `li.__tile.is-figure` (aria-hidden) > `__fig` > IsoKit `svg.__svg` (`g.__base`, `g.__new` with `__nb` beads and leaders, `g.__base2`, `__dim`, `g.__old` dashed with `__bad` bead and `__x`) + HTML `__lab` labels + `__figtag`.
- Fields: `items [{t, d, hold, link}]`, `drawing {step, reject, cut, remade, number}` (optional; `step` is the 1-based step the drawing shows; the labels are short aria-hidden duplicates of that step's text, `[placeholders]` allowed).
- Drawing: IsoKit pipes (`isoPipe`, `isoCylD` fusion beads, `isoDim`, `isoLeader`): a PP run whose rejected length is lifted clear above the gap (dashed, crossed), the remade length fused in place with its two new joints in `flow`. No figures on the dimension.
- Tokens: `navy` / `limestone` grounds (limestone tiles `data-theme="light"`), numerals 300 weight (176 px lead, 72 px others), hold tag mono in a `currentColor` frame, drawing on `surface-raised` with a `line-strong` frame.
- Phone: one column, the drawing right after the step it shows; lead numeral 112 px.
- Motion (`motion.js`): ported from Subframe Motion "Type under pressure" (type-press): tiles rise 16 px in reading order and each numeral presses up from a sliver (translateY 55 %, scale 0.12 × 0.55, 760 ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), 35 ms a glyph). The drawing then makes the repair: run draws, dimension appears, the rejected length lifts to its exploded place, the remade length drops in, its beads strike, labels follow (about 3.6 s). Pointing at or focusing the drawing or its step replays the repair.
- Not for: a sequence with no hold or drawing to show (Steps, HoldTimeline).
