How a defect is held and closed

  1. 01/

    Identify and hold

    Whoever finds it tags and segregates the item, or stops work, and raises an NCR.

  2. 02/

    Disposition

    Reject, rework or accept as is. Accepting as is, or an unapproved repair, needs your approval.

    Reported to you
