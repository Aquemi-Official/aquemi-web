RiserValves sets a table of response times by request on a navy sheet with one riser pipe: a valve per request tees into its first response, and a second branch runs under it and turns up into what follows, so the eye goes from request to response to follow-up.

Look `riser-valves` (`assets/src/blocks/look-riser-valves.js`), pass 9b; built from concept `research/pass9b/concepts/contact-service.2.png` (steel-blue riser with a valve per severity row branching out to the response and follow-up columns). Replaces the pass 9 look `response-lanes` (ResponseLanes, removed). Used on contact-service ("Response times by request").

- Data: a `table` block with `"look": "riser-valves"` (an `as: "router"` field may stay; the look replaces it). `cols`: [your request, first response, what follows] (column heads; on phones the zone keys). `rows`: [request, first response, what follows]; cells may be `{t, link, intent}`. Each value cell is split into lines at "; " outside brackets and parentheses. A request written "Severity N: …" shows "Severity N" over the rest; the first request without one opens under a stronger rule (`is-break`). `intro` prints in the section head.
- Markup: `section.aq-band--navy.aq-riser-valves-sec` → section head → `div.aq-riser-valves` → `p.__colh` (aria-hidden) and `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` (`is-sev`, `is-first-sev`, `is-top`, `is-end`, `is-break`; `data-sev`): `span.__riser` (aria-hidden riser segment), `div.__req` (`span.__no` mono index, `h3.__t` with `span.__sev` and `span.__what`), and two `div.__cell[data-zone]` with `p.__k` (zone key: visually hidden on desktop) and one `p.__v` per clause. Zone 1 holds `span.__br--a` (branch with an inline-SVG gate valve `svg.__valve`); zone 2 holds `span.__br--b` (`span.__run` under the first response, `svg.__elb` the elbow, `span.__rise` up to the text). Every `i.__w` is a water line.
- Geometry (desktop): request 30 %, gutter `space-24 + space-8` with the riser at its centre, then two equal answer columns. The riser is drawn per row (`top: 0; bottom: -1px`, navy fill hiding the row rule), so it is continuous whatever the row heights; it opens under a flange in the first row and closes at the last row's branch B. Branch A sits on the first line; branch B at the middle of the row's foot padding. Drawn parts are fixed-size SVGs (never scaled).
- Tokens: `navy` ground; walls `on-navy-muted` at 55 %; water `flow` mixed 78 % with `on-navy`; text `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted`; severity 1's label in `flow` mixed 40 % with `on-navy` (7.5:1 light, 9:1 dark).
- States: `data-rv="shut"` (set by motion only) empties the valve body and turns its lever across the pipe. Hover or focus within a row opens its valve (body `on-navy`, lever along the pipe, 200 ms) and shows both branches full in `on-navy`.
- Phones: the riser runs down a 64 px left gutter; request, first response and what follows stack; both branches are short tees into the zone keys (valve on branch A); the elbows and rises go.
- Not for timelines with real durations, or tables whose rows are not one route from a request to its answers (use a plain table).

## Motion

`motion.js`, the page's one scroll-linked story: ported from Subframe Motion "Scroll-driven image reveal" (scroll-window). One scroll progress over the rows (list top at 72 % of the viewport to its foot at 62 %) is fanned into staggered phases, phase_i = clamp((p − i / n) × n × 1.24), so each request completes before the next starts; each phase is split into the riser segment (0–0.35), valve and branch A (0.3–0.6; the valve opens at half), branch B's run (0.5–0.82), then elbow and rise (0.8–1), written as `--rv-r`, `--rv-a`, `--rv-b`, `--rv-c` on the row. Scrolling back drains it. The words enter once per row (12 px, 0.5 s) and never dim. Nothing under reduced motion: the CSS defaults are full pipes and open valves.
