How a loop works

Storage and distribution loops7 stages

  1. 01

    From generation

    PW enters the tank under a vent filter.

  2. 02

    Storage tank

    Buffers generation against peak demand and drains fully.

  3. 03

    Loop pump

    Keeps every section turbulent at peak demand.

  4. 04

    Heat exchanger

    Holds loop temperature. [heat exchanger, standard or option]

  5. 05

    Points of use

    Every outlet sits on a short branch, so no water stands.

  6. 06

    Return sensors

    Measure the water after the last outlet.

  7. 07

    Spray ball

    Wets the whole tank head so no surface stays dry.
