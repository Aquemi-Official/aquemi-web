Endotoxin margin starts at your PW feed

  1. 01

    A fixed factor, not a fixed level

    A still cuts endotoxin by a set number of logs, so what leaves it depends on what the Purified Water (PW) feed brings in.

  2. 02

    Many stills guarantee 2.5 to 3 log

    That is the reduction many still makers guarantee, according to the FDA Guide to Inspections of High Purity Water Systems.

  3. 03

    250 EU/mL in, 0.25 EU/mL out

    A 3 log still fed at 250 EU/mL delivers 0.25 EU/mL, which already fails the < 0.25 EU/mL limit.

  4. 04

    Still and feed designed as one

    The guaranteed reduction sets your PW endotoxin target in the functional specification, and we design the PW system to hold it.
