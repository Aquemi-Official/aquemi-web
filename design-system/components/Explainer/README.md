Explainer sets out one idea as a figure plus three or four short numbered callouts instead of paragraphs, for a mechanism a reader should see at a glance (endotoxin margin set by the feed, what an upgrade costs in requalification).

- Data: a `prose` block with `"as": "explainer"`, `points: [{t, d, at}]` (3–4; `d` 25 words or fewer) and an optional `figure`. Without `points` the block renders as plain Prose.
- Figures (no new artwork per page):
  - `{kind: "levels", limit: {v, unit, label}, bars: [{label, v, level, note, n}]}`: test-tube columns (open lip, rounded foot) drawn in 1.5 px `line-strong`, filled in `flow-tint` with a `flow` top rule, against a dashed `fail` limit rule whose label sits in a lane left of the tubes. Each bar is annotated from its own position: below the limit a `flow` dimension line from the fill to the limit marked "Margin"; at or above it the fill's top rule turns `fail` and "No margin" is written in `fail`. These two words are the only text the figure adds. A numeric `v` (in the limit's unit) sets the height and is printed on the plot; without `v`, `level` `"below"`, `"at"` or `"above"` draws the bar with no number. `n` puts that point's number on the bar. The plot is `aria-hidden`; each bar's `label` and `note` are the real text, so say the value in the note.
  - `{kind: "art", art: "<page id>"}`: that page's equipment drawing (Illustration, `ART` in `parts/Hero.js`), with each point's number pinned at its `at: [x%, y%]`.
  - No figure: the callouts alone, in one row.
- Markup (`blocks/prose.js`): full-width section head, then `.aq-explainer` > the figure in a Plate (`.aq-plate.aq-explainer__plate`, caption "Aquemi · <nav>") > `figure.aq-explainer__fig` (`.aq-levels` with `.aq-levels__plot` (`--at` the limit as a fraction), `.aq-levels__limit`, `.aq-levels__tube` (`--h`) > `.aq-levels__glass` > `.aq-levels__fill.is-below|is-at|is-above`, `.aq-levels__top` (marker, value), `.aq-levels__gap.is-margin|is-none`, `figcaption.aq-levels__keys`; or `.aq-explainer__fig--art` with `.aq-explainer__pin`), then `ol.aq-explainer__points` (`.aq-explainer__n`, `h3.aq-explainer__t`, `p.aq-explainer__d`).
- Without a figure (`aq-explainer--bare`) the points run in one row (up to four; five or more wrap three to a row), each under a 1 px `line` hairline. Phones: one column.
- Points: the index (`aq-index`, Geist Mono 13/24 `ink-subtle`) on its own line, head Geist 500 20/28 `ink` in sentence case (18/26 on phones; units such as EU/mL are wrapped in `.aq-explainer__unit` and keep their case), text 16/26 `ink-muted` (15/24). Hairlines `line` between points.
- Tokens: the Plate is a `line` frame on `surface-raised`; tubes `line-strong`; fill `flow-tint` with a `flow` top rule (`fail` at or above the limit); limit a dashed `fail` rule; markers are `flow` rings with `flow` digits on `surface`. Labels on the plot in Geist Mono 500 12/16, sentence case. No ticks, gradients or shadows. Desktop: plate left, callouts right; phones: plate first.
- Ground: the section's own `surface`; the figure's Plate provides the one surface change.
- Numbers only from the page. Never invent a value to give a bar a height: use `level`. Not for a list of choices (LinkGrid) or a sequence (ProcessSteps).

## Motion

Motion (`motion.js`): the figure makes the argument before the text. Levels: the dashed limit rule runs across, each tube fills to its value in turn (one at or over the limit overshoots a hair and settles), its number pin strikes and its value prints, then the margin dimension or "No margin" appears; the captions follow. Art: the drawing prints in and the pins strike in order. The numbered points rise one after another, in step with the figure. Without a figure the points simply rise in order. Under 4 s, once.
