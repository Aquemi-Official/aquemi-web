How a defect is held and closed

NCR
  1. 01

    Identify and hold

    Whoever finds it tags and segregates the item, or stops work, and raises an NCR.

  2. 02

    Disposition

    Reject, rework or accept as is. Accepting as is, or an unapproved repair, needs your approval.

    Hold Signed by you

  3. 03

    Repair

    A rejected product-contact joint is cut out and remade to [PP joint repair rule], under a new number.

  4. 04

    Root cause and CAPA

    Repeat, systemic or client-reported: cause found, procedure, training or supplier changed.

  5. 05

    Effectiveness check

    Evidence after an agreed period that the action worked, signed before the CAPA closes.

  6. 06

    Reported to you

    Every NCR logged in your turnover package; one after shipment reaches you within [NCR notice after shipment, working days].
