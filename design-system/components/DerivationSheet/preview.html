Water grade by process step

Common practice, not a rule: your risk assessment sets the grade per step.

  1. (1)
    Process step

    Intermediate rinses; final rinse, intact-skin devices

    What sets it

    Your cleaning validation; ISO 13485 7.5.2

    Typical water

    PW

  2. (2)
    Process step

    Final rinse, radiation or ethylene oxide sterilized

    What sets it

    ISO 11737-1 bioburden; ISO 11137-2 dose audits; ISO 11135

    Typical water

    PW with a microbial action level

  3. (3)
    Process step

    Final rinse, blood or lymph contact

    What sets it

    20.0endotoxin units per device

    USP <161> · tested to USP <85>

    Typical water

    PW with a written endotoxin limit, or Water for Injection (WFI)

  4. (4)
    Process step

    Final rinse, cerebrospinal fluid contact

    What sets it

    2.15endotoxin units per device

    USP <161>

    Typical water

    WFI, or water held to its endotoxin limit
