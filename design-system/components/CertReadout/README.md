CertReadout shows certification status as an instrument readout: a navy ruler-framed box with the selected item's standard set giant and light, beside the items as mono readout lines whose titles are buttons that put them on the readout.

- On the site: a `grid` block with `"look": "cert-readout"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-cert-readout.js`), cmp-quality "Certification status". Origin: pass 9b concept `cmp-quality.3` (ruler-framed readout box with a giant light numeral and a REQ tag, certifications as a 2 × 2 grid of mono status lines). Replaces CertSeals.
- Markup: `<div class="aq-cert-readout">` > `__box.aq-fx-host` (aria-hidden mirror; `--p` selected index, `--n` last index; `canvas.aq-fx-shader[data-gl="guilloche"]`, three `svg.__rule` (top, foot, side), `__no`, `__mark` > `span`, `__face` > `p.__big` (`is-long`), `p.__ft`, `p.__fd`) + `ol.__lines` > `li.__line` (`is-on`, `data-i`) > `h3.__k` > `button.__pick[aria-pressed]` (`__n`, `__t`), `p.__tag` (`is-long`), `p.__d`, `p.__go` > link.
- Fields: `items [{t, d, tag, icon, link}]` (`icon` unused here). Tags longer than 14 characters (placeholders) set in mono at body size.
- Behaviour: pointing at, focusing or clicking a line selects it (the look's own document handler, so it works without GSAP and under reduced motion): the face is replaced, `__no` and the marker label update, the marker glides down the side ruler (420 ms), the line's top rule fills `flow`.
- Tokens: box `navy` with `on-navy` / `on-navy-muted` rules and text; the reading 300 weight 104 px; lines `line-strong` top rule, selected `flow`; tag mono 28 px; links `flow`. FxGuilloche at `--fx-amt` 0.07 behind the reading.
- Phone: the readout over the lines, one line a row; reading 56 px.
- Motion: the reading is ported from Subframe Motion "Quote tick" (quote-tick): the new reading enters 8 px from the side of travel in 220 ms and its numeral fades from `flow-tint` over 800 ms (CSS keyframes). `motion.js`: rulers wipe on, the reading rises 16 px, lines follow 0.08 s apart; then one tour reads lines 2 to the last and returns to the first (0.7 s each), skipped once the visitor touches a line.
- Not for: standards a system is designed to (StandardsOrbit, ProofFolders).
