What a case study proves

Each published figure will carry four things your team can check.

  1. 01

    01Figure

    Measured value, unit and temperature, against the pharmacopoeia limit.

  2. 02

    02Method

    Sampling point, number of samples and period.

  3. 03

    03Report

    Executed acceptance test or qualification report, with its number.

  4. 04

    04Approval

    A second engineer checks it [confirm]; the client's quality unit approves the text in writing, deviations included.
