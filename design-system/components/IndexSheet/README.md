IndexSheet shows a document package's index as one printed sheet: a limestone sheet on the navy band with the section title set very large, every section numbered in a ruled two-column grid with all its documents in view, and a folded leaf at the right carrying one isometric line drawing of the system the package comes with.

- On the site: a `docs` block with `"look": "index-sheet"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-index-sheet.js`), cmp-documentation "The turnover package, section by section". Concept: `research/pass9b/concepts/cmp-documentation.0.png` (limestone sheet on navy, huge headline, numbered sections in a ruled grid, isometric pipe-frame drawing). Replaced SpineShelf (pass 9).
- Markup: section `aq-band--navy aq-index-sheet-sec` > `<div class="aq-index-sheet">` > `p.__strip` (aria-hidden: the block's `nav` and the section count) and `div.__sheet[data-theme="light"]` > the section head, then `__main` > `__index` > (Fold) `ol.__grid` > `li.__cell[.is-key]` (`__rule`, `__n` aria-hidden, `__body` > `h3.__t`, `p.__d`) and `__wing` > `__face` > `.aq-iso-figure.__fig` > `svg.aq-iso.__svg` (IsoKit: base frame, pipe-frame posts and rails, a flanged spool with a valve and a gauge, aria-hidden) + `span.__back` (aria-hidden, the leaf's blank back).
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `nav`, `items [{t, d, key, link}]`, `fold`, `fold_noun`. `d` is shown whole (placeholders as chips); `key` sets that section's number and rule in `flow`; `link` (optional) makes the title a link.
- Tokens: band `navy`, sheet `limestone` with `on-limestone` / `on-limestone-muted` text, rules `line-strong` hairlines (a dashed one is the leaf's crease), numbers in Geist Mono 300, titles Geist Mono 500 14 px, entries Geist 14 px; `flow` only for the key section and the water. The sheet is `data-theme="light"`, so it stays paper in the dark theme.
- Fold: phones only (desktop shows the whole index).
- Below 1100 px container width the leaf sits over the grid (the drawing centred); on phones the sheet keeps a navy margin, the drawing is small, the grid one column with its fold.
- Motion (`motion.js`): ported from Subframe Motion "Paper unfold reveal" (paper-fold): the leaf arrives folded over the index, its blank back up, and swings open on its crease (rotateY -180° to 0, 720 ms, cubic-bezier(.25,.75,.2,1)) as the sheet settles from a slight turn; the index rules itself in row by row, numbers and entries rising after their rule; then the drawing plays IsoKit's choreography on the opened leaf (frame back to front, the spool, the water runs it once). Once, under 4.5 s; no fold where the leaf sits over the grid; nothing under reduced motion.
- Not for: documents delivered by stage (StageStacks), a short list of documents (DocList), evidence mapped to standards (ProofFolders).
