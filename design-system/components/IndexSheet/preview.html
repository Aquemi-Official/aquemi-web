The turnover package, section by section

Documents handed over with your system.

  1. 01

    Index and project summary

    Register with current revisions, scope, tag list, connection points (battery limits)

  2. 02

    Requirements and design basis

    URS compliance matrix, basis of design, risk assessment, design review report, traceability matrix, commissioning and qualification (C&Q) plan

  3. 03

    Specifications

    Functional and design specifications, alarm list, Part 11 and Annex 11 assessment

  4. 04

    Drawings and calculations

    Piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs), general arrangement, isometrics, process flow diagram, balances, membrane projections, loop hydraulics

  5. 05

    Materials and components

    [PP material certificates] by lot, to [PP material standard]; [elastomer certificates per compound]; [certificates supplied for PVC parts]; media, resin and component certificates

  6. 06

    Fabrication and joining

    Fabrication and inspection plan; [PP joining method] procedure, welder qualifications, [PP joint records kept]

  7. 07

    Cleaning and drainability

    Slope survey; dead leg register to [dead leg design limit, L/D]; spray coverage test; cleaning certificate after [pre-commissioning flush and cleaning procedure for PP]

  8. 08

    Pressure equipment and panels

    Pressure tests to [PP pressure test method]. Canadian registration number (CRN) under CSA B51 and vessel data reports, where required. Panel certification or field evaluation

  9. 09

    Instruments and calibration

    Calibration certificates, traceable via [calibration provider and accreditation held]; total organic carbon system suitability, where an analyzer is fitted; vent filter integrity test

  10. 10

    Software

    Version and checksum record, code review, baseline backup, audit trail review, backup and restore procedure

  11. 11

    Testing and qualification

    Factory and site acceptance test (FAT, SAT), installation, operational and performance qualification (IQ, OQ, PQ) protocols and reports. Deviation logs, sanitization records.

  12. 12

    Operation, maintenance and training

    Operation and maintenance manual, draft operating procedures, spare parts list for the first [months of operation], training records
