From pipe to turnover

A joint inside a finished skid is hard to re-inspect.

J-01 [PP joining method]
  1. 01HP

    Fabrication and inspection plan

    Agreed before material is ordered: standards, inspection extent, records, hold and witness points.

    Hold point Signed by both parties

  2. 02

    Receive and verify material

    Each PP lot checked against its certificate and logged.

  3. 03WP

    Qualify the set-up

    Test joints made and accepted before any production joint.

    Witness point

  4. 04WP

    Join, record and inspect

    Each joint numbered and logged with its parameters; visual examination. Rejects cut out, renumbered.

    Witness point

  5. 05WP

    Test and survey

    Pressure test, slope survey and dead leg check.

    Witness point

  6. 06

    Clean and turn over

    Flushed and rinsed with PW before factory acceptance testing. Dossier indexed by joint number, updated with field joints.
