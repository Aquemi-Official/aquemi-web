What a maintenance visit covers

  1. 01

    Data review

    Conductivity, TOC, temperature and alarms since the last visit.

  2. 02

    Pretreatment

    Softener leakage, carbon or bisulfite, RO inlet chlorine.

  3. 03

    RO and DI/EDI

    Normalized flow, salt passage and pressure drop against baseline.

  4. 04

    Ultraviolet (UV) and sanitization

    Lamp intensity and hours; sanitization verified at the coldest point.

  5. 05

    Loop hydraulics

    Return flow against the qualified minimum, pump and valves.

  6. 06

    Wearing parts

    Seals and diaphragms like for like, lots recorded; vent filters tested.

  7. Sign-off

    Signed report

    As-found, as-left and return-to-service results, signed before we leave.
