Flow regime

≥ [design minimum return velocity, m/s]

Design value

  1. 02Slope and drainage

    ≥ [design minimum slope, in/ft or %]

    Design value
    Continuous fall to low-point drains
    Basis
    WHO TRS 1033, 7.2.1; fall measured on each run

  2. 03Dead legs

    ≤ [dead leg design limit, L/D]

    Basis
    WHO TRS 1033, 8.6; measured and listed in the dead leg register

    Basis
