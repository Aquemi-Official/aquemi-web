Router lets a buyer pick the situation they are in and see one route through what follows: the water they need, the system, and where to start with us.

- Data: a `table` block with `"as": "router"`. `cols`: the situation column (its head is not shown: each situation is a button), then one column per station ("Water you need", "System", "Start with"). `rows`: the situation, then one cell per station, a string or `{t, link, intent}` (as CompareTable). `intent` (optional): the options the linked page's form ticks on arrival, so "Send your URS" lands on contact-proposal with "I have a URS or a draft" ticked: `{"t": "Send your URS", "link": "contact-proposal", "intent": "I have a URS or a draft|Proposal against your URS"}`.
- Markup (`blocks/router.js`): `.aq-router` holds, per row, a `button.aq-router__opt` (`.aq-index`, `.aq-router__t`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) followed by its `.aq-router__route` (an `ol.aq-router__stations` of `li` > `.aq-router__k` label + `.aq-router__v` value). One route is open at a time, the first by default; closed routes stay in the markup with `hidden`.
- Drawn on a Plate (`wPlate`: a `line` frame on `surface-raised`, caption "Aquemi · <nav>"). The caption counts the rows only under a noun the block names: `caption_noun` (`"caption_noun": "roles"` on careers reads "Aquemi · Roles · 6 roles"), else `fold_noun`; with neither it prints no count, since a router is not always a set of routes and a bare count of its own rows says nothing to the reader. `"caption_noun": ""` drops the count even when the block has a `fold_noun` (the same rule as GradeLanes and RoleMatrix, `wCount` in `core.js`). Desktop: situations as rows on `line` hairlines on the left (Geist 500 16/24 `ink-muted`) with the shared mono index; the chosen one is the plate's one `flow-tint` row with a 2 px `flow` line on its left (text `ink`, index `flow`) and a 1 px `flow` line with an arrowhead into the route. The route spans every row in a `line` frame on `surface`: stations with no boxes, each a "Station 0N" `eyebrow`, then its column name (Geist 500 14/20 `ink-muted`) on a `line-strong` hairline and the value (Geist 16/24), joined by 1 px `flow` lines with arrowheads (the water path). Phones: the route opens under its situation (a short arrowed riser from the row), stations stacked with arrowed risers between them, the station number in mono before the column name.
- The station chain sits vertically centred in the route frame. Four or more stations lay out two per row (2 x 2 for four) on desktop, so each box keeps a readable measure; the station numbers carry the order across the turn. Phones: one column. Station links get a 44 px hit area on phones.
- Links: the station with the last `{t, link}` cell (usually "Start with") is the end station (`li.is-go`: a `flow` hairline and column name, on `flow-tint`), and the whole route frame is clickable to that page (`.aq-router__route.is-link` holds `a.aq-router__hit`, a pointer-only overlay with `tabindex=-1` and `aria-hidden`; the real link in the station stays the keyboard and screen reader target). The end cell's `intent` rides on both, so a click anywhere on the route ticks the same options. The situation button stays a toggle.
- Pending station: a value that opens with a `[placeholder]` ("[days]; DI runs until release") is a fact still to confirm. Its station (`li.is-pending`) gets a dashed hairline, its value in `ink-muted`, and a "To confirm" `eyebrow` (`.aq-router__tbc`, with a dashed dot), so it reads as a value on order rather than a gap. The end station never goes pending.
- Ground: the section's `surface`, with the plate on `surface-raised`, in either theme.
- Use it when the first column of a table is "your situation" and the rest is what to do about it. For a comparison of two routes use Versus; for a plain lookup with no "where to start", CompareTable.

## Motion

Motion (`motion.js`): situations slide in down the list; the chosen one's node pops (`--aq-node`) and its pipe runs into the route (`--aq-pipe`), the route frame opens from the pipe side, then the stations come on in order, each pipe growing to the next before it lands; the filled end station settles last. Picking another situation replays the chain for its route, quicker (about 1 s). Pipe and node variables default to 1 in the CSS, so the drawing is complete without motion. Hover nudges an option's index.
