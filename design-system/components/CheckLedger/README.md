CheckLedger sets a router table of checks a buyer can run before a case study exists as a ledger of tiles: each check is one row headed by a navy slash-numeral tile, then the route to arrange it, what you check, what it does not prove, and the time to arrange in mono at the right edge.

- On the site: Projects, "Verify before a case study exists" (`table`, `"as": "router"`, `"look": "check-ledger"`; renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-check-ledger.js`). Origin: pass 9b concept `research/pass9b/concepts/projects.0.png` (numeral tiles as row heads, mono lead times at the right edge, two blueprint fitting tiles as texture); replaces the pass 9 seam ledger.
- Markup: `div.aq-check-ledger` → `div.__head` (aria-hidden column names "01 / Route" … in visual order, `span.__hc[data-col]`) → `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` → `div.__n` (navy tile, `span.__nv` "01/"), `div.__c--t` (`h3.__t`, what you check; first in the DOM, placed third), `div.__c--go` (`a.__go`, the route with the row's `intent`), `div.__c--no` (`p.__no`), `div.__c--time` (`p.__time`). Every tile carries its column name as `span.__k`. Fitting tiles: `span.__fit` (aria-hidden IsoKit drawing on navy) in a tile's right edge.
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `cols` [check, route, does not prove, time], `rows` (cells strings or `{t, link, intent}`), `fold`, `fold_noun`. New optional field `fits`: `{"<row index>": [route tile fig, does-not-prove tile fig]}`, figs `cross`, `tee`, `valve` (PP fittings; decoration only, `null` for none).
- Tokens: `surface-raised` tiles on `line` / `line-strong` hairlines, the time tile on `surface`; `navy` numeral tiles with `on-navy` Geist Light 64 numerals; check and not-proved text Geist Light `ink`; routes `ink` with a `flow` arrow; column names mono `ink-muted`. Square corners.
- Phones: one card per check: the numeral tile and the route share the top strip; what you check, what it does not prove and the time stack under it, each labelled.
- Not for situation-to-page routers (ManifoldRouter, CheckerRouter, StairRouter).

## Motion

Motion: the column names decode; each row's navy tile wipes in and its numeral turns over on split-flap leaves (ported from Subframe Motion departure-board: rotateX 0 to -180 deg, 440 ms, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), 45 ms apart; the real text is restored); the row's tiles rise left to right and fitting tiles wipe up. Once, under 3 s. Pointing at a row turns its numeral over again (0.3 s). Pointing at a tile lays a crosshair over the grid (ported from chart-crosshair: row rule and column rule glide 200 ms power3, the column name lights, the tile tints `flow-tint`); keyboard focus on a route snaps the row rule. Nothing under reduced motion.
