CheckLedger sets a router table of checks a buyer can run before a case study exists as a two-sided ledger on a navy band: what each check lets you see on the left, what it does not prove on the right, and the route to arrange it on the seam between them.

- On the site: Projects, "Verify before a case study exists" (`table`, `"as": "router"`, `"look": "check-ledger"`; renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-check-ledger.js`). Origin: the seam-as-boundary idea of `76-p9-systems-2` (battery-limit split, calm hybrid) and the fill-versus-outline ownership of `48-concept-roles`.
- Markup: `div.aq-check-ledger` → `div.__head` (aria-hidden column names: a filled square for the first column, an outlined square for the third) → `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` → `div.__l` (`span.__n` outline numeral, `h3.__t`), `div.__m` (`span.__pipe` with `span.__flow`, `span.__node`, `a.__go` the route as a square limestone button with the row's `intent`, `p.__time`), `div.__r` (`p.__no` with an outlined `i.__sq`). Each cell also carries its column name as `span.__k` (visually hidden on wide screens, shown on phones).
- Fields: `h`, `intro`, `cols` [check, route, does not prove, time], `rows` (cells strings or `{t, link, intent}`), `fold`, `fold_noun`. Any four-column router of the same meaning can use it.
- Tokens: `navy` band; `on-navy` check text (Geist Light 26), `on-navy-muted` for what is not proved, numerals as outline text; `limestone` buttons with `on-limestone` labels, `radius-sm`; placeholders as limestone-tinted chips. Hover: the row's node fills limestone.
- Phones: one card per check, the seam a rule down the left edge carrying the node; route button, time ("Time to arrange:") and the labelled "does not prove" stack under the check.
- Not for situation-to-page routers (ManifoldRouter, CheckerRouter, StairRouter).

## Motion

Motion: the column heads drop in; each row's two sides slide in from opposite edges and meet at the seam while the route button presses in on its node. Then water runs down the seam row by row, each node filling limestone as it passes and settling back. Once, under 4 s; nothing under reduced motion; folded rows appear whole when opened.
