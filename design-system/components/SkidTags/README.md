SkidTags keys an evidence table to an elevation drawing of a Purified Water skid: numbered square tags stand round the drawing with a leader to the part each standard governs, and the table rows below carry the same numbers, so every standard points at its part.

- On the site: a `table` block with `"look": "skid-tags"` (renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-skid-tags.js`). Origin: pass 9b concept `research/pass9b/concepts/compliance.4.png` (skid elevation with five numbered tags above a four-column standards table). Replaced PipeCollage (pass 9, no concept of its own).
- Markup: `<section class="aq-section aq-band--limestone aq-skid-tags-sec" data-theme="light">` → `div.aq-skid-tags` (`data-first`, then `data-k` = the chosen row) with `div.__top` (the section head beside `div.__stage`: `div.__fig` 2:1 holding `svg.__svg` (aria-hidden: `g.__ground`, `g.__plant` with `g.__part[data-part]` for the tagged parts, `g.__water`, `g.__lead[data-part]` leader + dot), `span.__crossbox` (clips) > `span.__cross` and one `button.__tag` per tagged row; then `p.__detail`, aria-live) and the table (`table.__table`: a visually hidden number column header, then the four column names; each `tr.__row[data-k][data-part]` has `td.__n` with `button.__num`, `th.__std` (row header), `td.__how`, `td.__proof`, `td.__go`, each cell with a `__k` column name for phones), inside the fold.
- Fields, as a table: `cols` [standard, what we design to, proof, where we show it], `rows` (cells strings or `{t, link}`), `fold`, `fold_noun`. New optional fields: `parts`, one per row, the drawn part that row's standard governs: `vessel` (pressure vessel with its nameplate), `panel` (control panel with its label plate), `deadleg` (a branch off the product line up to a valve, its length dimensioned L), `wall` (a broken-out length of product-contact pipe showing the wall), `joint` (a fusion bead on a pipe joint), each at most once, a row without one gets no tag; `tag`, the name of the number column and the drawing's tag group ("On the drawing"). The drawing is fixed line art and names no standard: which row points at which part is data, and a part must only be given to a row whose own copy ties it there.
- Drawing: elevation, drawn inline (not IsoKit, which is isometric) in its hand: `line-strong` 1.5 px non-scaling outlines filled with the paper (`limestone`), 1 px details, water as one `flow` centre line through fitted pipe pieces (straight lengths and elbow fittings, joints showing). Polypropylene skid only: no stainless finish, no still.
- Selection (Subframe Motion atlas-hotspots, ported): a tag, a row number, pointing at a row or focusing into it chooses that standard; its tag and number turn `flow` and grow to 112 % (180 ms), its row gets a `flow` edge, its part and leader turn `flow`, and the crosshair ring glides to the part (250 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)). Arrow keys, Home and End step through the tags or the numbers. The selection persists; the first tagged row starts chosen. Instant under reduced motion. Code chips from StandardsOrbit land on the row (`tr`) and so light its tag too.
- Tokens: `limestone` band, `navy` tags with `on-navy` mono numbers, `flow` for water and the chosen standard, `line` row rules, `line-strong` header rule, `ink`/`ink-muted` text, `surface-raised` (60 %) under the chosen row, `flow-tint` for a StandardsOrbit landing.
- Desktop shows every row; the fold applies on phones only. Phones: heading, drawing (tags stay 44 px targets), the readout of the chosen row under it (number, standard, what we design to), then each row as a card led by its number, column names in mono above proof and link.
- Use it for three to five engineering or material standards that each govern one visible part of the skid. For documents pinned to the skid, use DocHotspots; for standards without parts, a plain evidence table.

## Motion

Motion: the elevation is plotted (ground and frame, then each piece in drawing order, strokes drawn on), the water runs the pipe runs in flow order, then each standard is tagged in number order: its dot appears, the leader draws out and the square tag is set at its end; the crosshair ring lands last. The table head fades in and the rows rise 12 px, 80 ms apart, as the table arrives. Both once, under 3 s; nothing under reduced motion (the selection still works, instantly).
