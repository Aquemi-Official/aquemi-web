URS-012

conductivity ≤ 1.3 µS/cm @ 25 °C

  1. URS compliance matrix

    Met Outlet designed to [outlet conductivity guarantee, µS/cm @ 25 °C]; return monitored.

  2. Basis of design

    Reverse osmosis and electrodeionization, passes set by your feed water analysis

  3. Functional specification

    Water above the divert setpoint goes to drain until conductivity recovers

  4. Verification
    • FAT divert on a simulated signal.
    • SAT and OQ your water, uncompensated reading.
