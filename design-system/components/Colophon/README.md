Colophon sets a spec block of company facts as a split ledger: a navy band with a limestone strip down its left edge, the section title set very large across the strip's edge so the colour split runs through the type and on down through the facts (item on the strip, fact and evidence on the navy).

- On the site: About, "Company facts" (`spec` block, `"look": "colophon"`; renderer `assets/src/blocks/look-colophon.js`). Origin: pass 9b concept `research/pass9b/concepts/about.0.png` (a giant cropped "Company facts" straddling a limestone and navy split, the fact table across it). The concept's small skid drawing is left out: the next section on the page draws the skid.
- Markup: `div.aq-colophon` → `span.__strip` (aria-hidden limestone strip, out to the page edge), `div.__mast` (the section head, then `p.__ghost`: an aria-hidden copy of the title in navy, clipped to the strip), `div.__head` (aria-hidden column names from `cols`), the fold with `ol.__rows` of `li.__row` (`.is-hl` for the highlighted row) → `div.__p` (`span.__n` "01 /", `h3.__pt` the item), `div.__v` (`p.__va` the fact; `p.__vz` the highlighted row's following sentence), `p.__m` (`span.__ev` evidence, `a.__go` link), then the block note.
- Fields: `h`, `caption`, `intro`, `cols` [item, fact, evidence], `rows [{p, v, m, hl, link, link_label, intent}]`, `note`, `fold`, `fold_noun`. The `hl` row's `v` is split at its first full stop (words unchanged).
- Custom properties: `--cph-w` strip width, `--cph-fs` title size, `--cph-p` divider position from 0 to 1 (1 at rest; motion sweeps it).
- Tokens: `aq-band--navy`; title Geist Light at display size, `limestone` on navy and `navy` on the strip; items mono `on-limestone`; facts Geist Light `on-navy`, evidence `on-navy-muted`; placeholders as limestone-tinted chips. Contrast holds in both themes (limestone and navy keep light-theme values).
- Phones: the strip narrows to the number column; item, fact and evidence stack on the navy beside it.
- Not for limits or design values with units (LimitStack, ParamTiles) or for terms a reader compares across options (TermsSheet, CompareTable).

## Motion

Motion: while the title lands (core word reveal) the strip waits off the page edge; then the divider sweeps in to its place (1.1 s power3.inOut) and the title's letters on the strip turn navy as it passes: one divider position drives both the strip and the copy's clip, the mechanism of Subframe Motion image-comparison (`inset(0 (100 - position)% 0 0)`). Then the rows follow: number and item, the fact wiping in, the evidence. Once, under 3 s. Opening the fold grows each new row from no height (ported from expandable-ledger: 210 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 30 ms apart; a keyboard press opens instantly). Nothing under reduced motion.
