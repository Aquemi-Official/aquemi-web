Explainer sets out one idea as a figure plus three or four short numbered callouts instead of paragraphs, for a mechanism a reader should see at a glance (endotoxin margin set by the feed, what an upgrade costs in requalification).

- Data: a `prose` block with `"as": "explainer"`, `points: [{t, d, at}]` (3–4; `d` 25 words or fewer) and an optional `figure`. Without `points` the block renders as plain Prose.
- Figures (no new artwork per page):
  - `{kind: "levels", limit: {v, unit, label}, bars: [{label, v, level, note, n}]}`: test-tube columns (open lip, rounded foot) with a tick scale, filled against a dashed `fail` limit rule whose label sits in a lane left of the tubes, in `fail`. Each bar is annotated from its own position: below the limit a `flow` dimension line from the fill to the limit marked "Margin"; at or above it "No margin" in `fail`. These two words are the only text the figure adds. A numeric `v` (in the limit's unit) sets the height and is printed on the plot; without `v`, `level` `"below"`, `"at"` or `"above"` draws the bar with no number. `n` puts that point's number on the bar. The plot is `aria-hidden`; each bar's `label` and `note` are the real text, so say the value in the note.
  - `{kind: "art", art: "<page id>"}`: that page's equipment drawing (Illustration, `ART` in `parts/Hero.js`), with each point's number pinned at its `at: [x%, y%]`.
  - No figure: the callouts alone, in two columns.
- Markup (`blocks/prose.js`): full-width section head, then `.aq-explainer` > the figure on a cream drawing plate (`.aq-plate.aq-explainer__plate[data-theme=light]`, title block "Aquemi | <nav>") > `figure.aq-explainer__fig` (`.aq-levels` with `.aq-levels__plot` (`--at` the limit as a fraction), `.aq-levels__limit`, `.aq-levels__tube` (`--h`) > `.aq-levels__glass` > `.aq-levels__fill.is-below|is-at|is-above`, `.aq-levels__top` (marker, value), `.aq-levels__gap.is-margin|is-none`, `figcaption.aq-levels__keys`; or `.aq-explainer__fig--art` with `.aq-explainer__pin`), then `ol.aq-explainer__points` (`.aq-explainer__n`, `h3.aq-explainer__t`, `p.aq-explainer__d`).
- Without a figure (`aq-explainer--bare`) the points run in one row (up to four; five or more wrap three to a row), each on a 2 px `ink` rule with a `flow` node, number 72/72, head 16/24, text 16/26 `ink`. Phones: one column, number 44/48.
- Points: number Geist Mono 300 44/48 in `flow` on its own line, head Geist Mono 600 15/22 uppercase `ink` (units such as EU/mL keep their case through `.aq-explainer__unit`), text 15/24 `ink-muted`. Phones: number 32/36.
- Tokens: plate cream (`surface` under light tokens) with ruler ticks; tubes and scale `line-strong`; fill `flow` at 45 % (70 % once it reaches the limit) with a `flow` top rule, `fail` at or above the limit; markers `flow` discs with `surface` digits. Desktop: plate left, callouts right; phones: plate first.
- Ground: the navy page (the shape is not in the light-sheet list); it also reads on a light sheet with `ground: "light"`.
- Numbers only from the page. Never invent a value to give a bar a height: use `level`. Not for a list of choices (LinkGrid) or a sequence (ProcessSteps).
