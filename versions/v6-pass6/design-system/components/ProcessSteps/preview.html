From protocol to release, then SAT

  1. 01

    Draft protocol

    From the approved functional specification, sent [protocol issue before FAT, weeks] ahead. Every test case has an acceptance criterion and a URS reference.

  2. 02

    Your review and approval

    Your validation team comments and may add test cases (Annex 15, 2.6); we answer in writing. Nothing runs before your quality unit signs.

  3. 03

    Internal dry run

    We run the full protocol ourselves first and fix what it finds, so the witnessed FAT confirms the system rather than debugs it.

  4. 04

    Witnessed execution

    On our floor, remote or both. Results recorded as found, raw data attached, fabrication hold points already closed.

  6. 05

    Deviations and punch list

    A failed acceptance criterion is a deviation: investigated, fixed, retested (Annex 15, 2.8). A defect that changes no result goes on the punch list.

  7. 06

    Report and release to ship

    Signed by both parties. Open deviations ship only on a quality-unit-approved no-impact assessment. Skid drained, then [post-FAT drying and sealing method].

    Hold Signed by both partiesSign:

  8. 07

    SAT on your site

    Critical tests re-run on your feed water, utilities and loop, conductivity read uncompensated (USP <645> stage 1). Then handed to IQ/OQ.

AquemiProcess

What leaves each stage

Your URS is the reference throughout (Annex 15, 3.2); every requirement keeps its ID.

  1. 01

    URS review and design

    Compliance matrix, basis of design, P&IDs, functional specification, DQ.

    Hold Signed by your quality unit

  2. 02

    Fabrication

    Material log with 3.1 certificates, weld maps, borescope, finish and pressure test records.

  3. 03

    FAT

    Approved protocol, raw data, deviation log, report. Release to ship.

    Hold Signed by both parties

  4. 04

    Installation and SAT

    Installation records, SAT on your utilities, leveraged tests flagged, SAT report.

    Hold Signed by you

  5. 05

    IQ and OQ

    IQ and OQ protocols and reports, draft procedures, training records.

    Hold Signed by your quality unit

  6. 06

    PQ, at least 12 months

    Protocol, sampling plan, phase reports, alert and action levels. Water released by phase.

  7. 07

    Service

    Signed visit reports, calibration certificates as found and as left, annual system review.

  8. 08

    Upgrade or retrofit

    Gap list and options, each with downtime and requalification scope.
