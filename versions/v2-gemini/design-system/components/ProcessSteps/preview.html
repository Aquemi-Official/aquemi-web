How a project runs

  1. Design

    Your URS turned into a functional specification, P&IDs and a risk assessment.

  2. Fabricate

    316L stainless steel, orbital welds with weld logs, finishes to ASME BPE.

  3. Test

    A wet FAT in our shop with your team present, then SAT after installation.

  4. Qualify

    IQ/OQ protocols written to GAMP 5 and executed with your validation team.

  5. Support

    Maintenance, calibration and spare parts that keep the system qualified.
