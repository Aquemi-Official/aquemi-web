What changed in 2017

USP has long allowed WFI from any process equivalent or superior to distillation.

The European Pharmacopoeia did not. In April 2017 the revised Ph. Eur. monograph 0169 began to allow WFI by reverse osmosis, single or double pass, coupled with other suitable techniques such as EDI, ultrafiltration or nanofiltration.

The quality limits did not change. Membrane WFI must meet the same conductivity, TOC and endotoxin limits as distilled WFI. What changed is the evidence inspectors expect: a contamination control strategy, as EU GMP Annex 1 describes, that shows how the system controls microbes and endotoxins without the heat of distillation.
