GradeLanes shows the minimum water grade step by step: each step drops a tap into one of three lanes, Potable, PW and WFI.

- Data: a `table` block with `"as": "grades"`. The grade column is the first whose name says grade or water (or `grade`: its index). The step is the first other column; further columns show as quieter lines.
- Markup (`blocks/grades.js`): `.aq-grades` > `.aq-grades__head` (step column name and lane names) and `ol.aq-grades__rows` of `li.aq-grades__row`: `.aq-index`, `.aq-grades__step` (`h3.aq-grades__t`, `p.aq-grades__d`, the grade text in mono `flow` as `p.aq-grades__g`), and `.aq-grades__lanes` (`role="img"`, labelled with the grade text) of three `.aq-grades__lane` with `.aq-grades__tap`. The first grade named gets a filled `flow` tap (`is-min`), an alternative an open one (`is-alt`). Lanes are double-walled `line-strong` pipes the full height of the list under framed lane caps (PW and WFI caps in `flow`). A `flow` branch pipe runs from the step to its tap (`.aq-grades__lanes.is-hit-0|1|2`). A grade cell that names no grade but says "same" ("Same grade as the product") gets `.is-same`: a dashed `flow` header across all three lanes, a dashed open tap on each and a `= product` mark (`.aq-grades__same`); the label stays the cell text. Drawn on a Plate (`wPlate`, title block Aquemi | nav | N steps). On phones the lane caps turn vertical.
- Lanes are `space-16` wide on desktop, `space-8` on phones, so phones keep the at-a-glance view. Desktop: the step text is capped at 35rem and the lanes sit at the right; the branch to each tap runs as a 2 px dotted `flow` leader from the text column, so no row has an empty middle.
- Ground: the navy page.

## Motion

`motion.js`: the lane caps drop onto the header rule, then water runs down the three lane pipes row by row; as it passes each step the step reads in, its branch fills from the step across to its grade and the tap there pops (the minimum grade with an overshoot, alternatives and "= product" taps more quietly). The fills are CSS transforms driven by `--aq-fill` (pipe, scaleY) and `--aq-branch` (branch, scaleX) on each row; both default to 1, so the drawing is complete without script and under reduced motion.
