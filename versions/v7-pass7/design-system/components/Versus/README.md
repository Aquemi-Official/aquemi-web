Versus sets two routes face to face across a spine of factors, for a buyer choosing one of two (hot or ambient WFI, multi-effect or vapour compression).

- Data: a `table` block with `"as": "versus"` and exactly three columns: `cols` [factor label, route A, route B], `rows` [factor, A, B]. A row whose factor starts "Fits when" or "Choose" becomes the verdict.
- Markup (`blocks/versus.js`): `.aq-versus` > `.aq-versus__head` (two `.aq-versus__name` and the `.aq-versus__vs` pill), `dl.aq-versus__rows` of `.aq-versus__row` (`dt` factor, two `dd` with `data-k` = route name), then `.aq-versus__verdict` with "Choose A when" and "Choose B when".
- Drawn on a Plate (`wPlate`, title block Aquemi | nav | N factors). Desktop: the route names large and uppercase (B in `flow`), joined by rules to a round "or" node on the spine; each factor is a numbered node on the `line-strong` spine (`.aq-versus__n`, large mono `flow` number, uppercase label) with leader lines out to A (right-aligned) and B (left-aligned, `flow` leader); verdicts in `line-strong` frames, B's in `flow`. Phones: names and node on top, the verdicts next, then the spine runs down the left edge with each factor's number on it, A and B under the factor, each tagged with its route and ruled on its left.
- Conclusion (block field `conclusion`: `[label, A, B]`, or a string for one line): a closing line that is not a choice, such as what carries into IQ/OQ (svc-fat-sat). Drawn as a footer band across the plate under the factors and the verdict (`.aq-versus__end`): `surface-sunken` between `line-strong` rules, the label boxed in mono caps on the spine, A right-aligned and B left-aligned in Geist 500 16/24. A string (or a one-cell array) spans the band under an "Either way" label. Phones: the label, then A and B each ruled and tagged with its route, like the factors.
- Ground: the navy page by default. `ground: "light"` sets it on the cream sheet with the reading blocks around it: the plate turns `surface-sunken` (`.aq-versus-plate--light`, set by the renderer) so it still reads as a drawing on the sheet, and the spine nodes, names and "or" pill take that ground; the verdict and conclusion frames go `surface`. Use it where a navy Versus would sit between two other navy sections. Keep each cell to one line of fact (≤ 25 words). Three or more routes: CompareTable.
- Supports `fold: N` (first N factors show; the verdict always shows). Put the deciding factors first.

## Motion

`motion.js`: the "or" node pops on the spine and the two route names slide in from either side; the spine then draws down factor by factor (a clip on the rows), each node lighting as the line reaches it and its two answers pushing out to their sides; only then does the verdict land, and the conclusion band opens from the spine outwards. Phones: names, then the verdict (it sits on top there), then the spine runs down the left edge one factor at a time. Under 5 s; nothing under reduced motion.
